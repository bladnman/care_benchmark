## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 40.0 / 54.0 weighted points (74.1%), while affective whys recovered 66.0 / 98.0 (67.3%). The planner and reconstruction were especially strong on server authority, privacy boundaries, sync, and deletion. Affective rationales leaked when the rule survived but the relationship-shaping reason was not carried, notably F8, F29, and F34.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-2 whys did better (36.0 / 44.0, 81.8%) than weight-3 whys (70.0 / 108.0, 64.8%). The multi-layer rows repeatedly kept the primary rule but dropped the secondary or downstream consequence, for example F2, F4, F12, F14, F16, F20, F27, and F40.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was higher (85.7%) than feature-level fidelity (66.1%). The plan was strong at carrying product philosophy, but the frozen reconstruction compressed many feature-level whys into implementation summaries.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes survived most often. Secondary contributors such as single-signal failure modes (F1/F13), stat-management exceptions (F8), catalog-choice optimization (F29), and queue-overwhelm in narration (F40) were the common dropouts. Downstream consequences were recovered when architectural risk was concrete (F15/F17/F18/F35), but affective slippage cascades were often compressed.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and privacy scored well because the plan made their architecture and trust boundaries explicit. Audio and accessibility were mixed: core mechanics survived, but deeper affective whys like procedural audio as the product spine and narration pacing as queue protection were partly compressed. Social was strong on controls and anti-attention surfaces.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Four included whys were marked `rule_without_why`: F5, F8, F29, and F34. Nineteen included whys were less than full because a rationale layer present in the plan was not reconstructed with enough exact evidence. No ungrounded reconstruction claims were counted.

What does the failure shape suggest? The planner produced a near-complete build plan and preserved the major product philosophy. The information loss happened mostly during compression of why-rich feature rules into implementation summaries: downstream affective consequences and exception rationales are the fragile payload.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound v06 operator. It cleanly separates strong feature capture (116 / 120) from lower feature-level intent recovery (66.1%). Without the evidence gate, this run would look nearly saturated.
- Add more targeted feature-level whys for areas where plans preserve rules but compress relationship rationale. F8, F29, and F34 were useful discriminators because the mechanism survived while the why vanished.
- Preserve multi-layer scoring for weight-3 whys. The primary layer often survives, but secondary contributors and downstream consequences are where compression appears. That made F1, F2, F12, F16, F20, F27, and F40 informative.
- Consider adding a few more affective whys tied to concrete implementation choices. Functional trust-boundary whys were easier to recover; affective exception whys exposed more plan-to-reconstruction loss.
- Keep the system-level cross-cutting bar, but require concise evidence trails in reports. In this run the bar was easy to satisfy for most principles, while S1 and S2 still showed useful loss in deeper layers.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The scorer was started with fresh context and the frozen reconstruction was not modified. I did read the repo-mandated phase-two instructions before the slot packet, then stayed inside the supplied allowlists.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance estimate. The numbers should be interpreted as one sample for this candidate/effort configuration.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on #22 mood-shaped idle motion, #57 evening palette shift, and #104 call captions toggle. The hard misses were #34 absence-length greeting variation, #35 boldness greeting variation, #58 night state, and #87 privacy-policy link. These borderlines did not materially change the conclusion.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S1 and S2 were cross-cutting in the plan, but received partial recovery because their deeper multi-layer consequences were compressed in reconstruction. Other system whys met both the reconstruction and plan-grounding bars.
- **Confabulation cases.** None were counted. The reconstruction reads as plan-derived, and no recovery was awarded for content unsupported by the plan.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several plausible semantic recoveries were denied or capped because only the rule survived, not the gold why: F5, F8, F29, and F34 were the clearest cases.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** The mechanism survived without rationale in four feature-level rows, mainly where the plan itself stated an invariant but compressed the relationship reason behind it.
