## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 12.0/32.0 and affective whys recovered 24.0/70.0. The difference is small; both leaked heavily because the plan often gave mechanisms without rationale.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 32.0/84.0, better than weight-2 whys at 4.0/18.0. The richer architecture sections gave the reconstructor partial layers, while many single-layer exceptions were either unreachable or rule-only.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 10.0/28.0 and feature-level fidelity was 26.0/74.0. The split is nearly even, which suggests the plan compressed both philosophy and feature exceptions rather than preserving one layer well.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary causes were recovered most often. Secondary contributors survived mainly in server/sync rows. Downstream consequences were routinely dropped, especially product-identity failure modes such as stale software, social comparison, and accessibility degradation.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Server-canonical sync was the strongest area: S7, F17, and F18 all reached partial. Social/privacy was weakest: S8, F20, F21, F23, F37, F38, and F39 lacked the private-relationship rationale.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Eight included whys were marked `rule_without_why`. F26 was the clearest plan-only case: PLAN.md links the performance budget to an "already in motion" feel, but the frozen reconstruction says Time-to-First-Bird is not recoverable.

The failure shape suggests a planner that can assemble a plausible implementation outline, but often loses the PRD’s affective and ethical reasons for refusing obvious product patterns.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The frozen reconstruction was read-only and was not modified. No PRD files, peer slots, or other waves were read.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run in one wave; it provides no candidate-level variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive calls affected features 2, 9, 31, 39, 48, 56, 62, 66, 90, and 104. Several are thin captures, but the fidelity denominator/recovery ledger prevents them from becoming unearned why credit.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The most subjective calls were S3, S5, and S9, where the plan preserved naturalist/accessibility mechanisms but not the full gold rationale.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction was counted. The reconstruction mostly stayed close to PLAN wording, sometimes too close to mechanism-only wording.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F9, F13, F21, F23, F31, F37, and F40 were denied recovery because exact rationale evidence was absent despite captured rules.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** Eight included whys had rules without the benchmark-relevant why, which is the central operational finding of this run.
