## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered **34.0 / 54.0** weighted credit (63.0%). Affective whys recovered **45.0 / 96.0** (46.9%). The gap came from social/relationship whys such as F22, F23, F38, and F39, and from anti-obligation whys such as F28 and F29.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-4 whys recovered **60.0 / 108.0** (55.6%), better than weight-2 whys at **19.0 / 42.0** (45.2%). Larger cross-cutting mechanisms tended to leave more reconstructable traces.
- **System vs feature.** System-level fidelity was **67.9%**, feature-level fidelity **49.2%**. Broad architecture and philosophy survived better than precise feature rationale.
- **Multi-layer pattern.** Primary rules were usually recoverable; downstream consequences were often missing. S1, S2, F10, F14, F15, F27, and F30 all lost one or more consequence layers.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Thirteen included whys were marked rule-without-why. The rule survived for the seven-bird cap, mood-shaped motion, sync-error tone, no leaderboards, starter birds, account export, visit log, and actual-aviary visits, but the gold why did not.

What this suggests: the candidate plan is strong as an implementation plan but compresses many affective reasons into operational rules. The frozen reconstruction then faithfully recovers the operational shape while losing several reasons the benchmark treats as product-defining.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted exception whys like F29-F40. They exposed mechanism-only planning that a pure feature checklist would miss.
- Add more downstream-consequence checks. The most common layer loss was not the rule or immediate cause, but the consequence of violating it.
- Preserve the evidence-bound operator. It usefully separated implemented rules from recoverable intent.
- Consider more social/privacy relationship whys. These discriminated well without needing a larger app surface.
- Keep the system cross-cutting bar, but require scorers to list the exact inherited decisions as this report does; it keeps partial/full calls auditable.

---

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This phase-2B pass used only the allowed phase-two package and run 001 artifacts. The frozen reconstruction was not modified.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one candidate run, with no variance estimate.
- **Borderline capture.** Feature 57 was counted captured inclusively; evening/warm palette and audio-ramp behavior were present, but the day-cycle call-quieting aspect was thin.
- **System cross-cutting.** S1, S2, and S6 were partial because they were cross-cutting and identified, but one or more multi-layer rationales were absent.
- **Confabulation.** No material ungrounded-reconstruction cases were counted. The dominant failure was omission or mechanism-only recovery.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several plausible semantic recoveries were denied because exact reconstruction evidence did not recover the gold why, only the rule.
