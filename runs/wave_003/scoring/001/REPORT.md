## 3. Diagnostic patterns

**Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better: 38.0 / 50 (76.0%) versus affective whys at 51.0 / 96 (53.1%). The strongest preserved functional material was architecture: S7, S8, F17, F18, F20, F26, and F36. Affective rationales around social restraint and relationship framing were much more likely to degrade to rules: F21, F22, F23, F33, F38, and F39.

**Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 68.0 / 104 (65.4%), while weight-2 whys recovered 21.0 / 42 (50.0%). The heavier whys tended to have more implementation consequences in the PLAN, which gave the reconstruction more to work with.

**System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 75.0%, much higher than feature-level fidelity at 57.6%. The PLAN preserved philosophy cross-cuttingly, but the reconstruction often compressed targeted F29-F40 rationales into mechanisms.

**Multi-layer recovery.** Primary implementation-cause layers survived most often. Downstream consequence layers were most fragile: S1 missed staleness/leaving, F3 missed quieter-not-mistrust, F15 missed the server-snapshot cause, F16 missed retrofit risk, F30 missed economy erosion, and F40 missed screen-reader queue overload.

**Subdomain patterns.** Simulation/sync/privacy and accessibility rendering were strong. Social/visit, account export/deletion, and observer-record notebook rationales were weak. Audio was mixed: F4 recovered no-canned/procedural and fallback consequences but not chorus phase-canceling.

**Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter v06 evidence gate materially lowered scores for rules with thin rationale: F19, F21, F22, F23, F31, F33, F34, F35, F38, and F39. These would be tempting semantic near-misses under a looser operator, but the frozen reconstruction did not carry the why.

What the failure shape suggests: Minimax M3 produced a highly buildable engineering plan and an orderly reconstruction, but it compresses affective product intent into operational constraints. The model is good at preserving architecture-as-causality and weaker at preserving relationship-as-causality.
