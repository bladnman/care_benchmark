## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 55.6% by weight; affective whys recovered 52.1%. Functional architecture such as F18 and F36 survived cleanly, while affective exceptions such as F22, F23, F29, F33, F38, and F39 often became rule-only.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 51.9%, lower than weight-2 whys at 57.1%. Multi-layer rows commonly kept L1 and dropped the temptation/downstream-consequence layers.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (64.3%) exceeded feature-level fidelity (50.8%). The plan carried slogans and broad principles well; feature-local why precision leaked first.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Server/sync, performance, and reduced-motion accessibility were strongest. Social, visit logging, account export/deletion, and no-announcement/no-comparison exceptions were weakest.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Several rows looked semantically close until v06 required exact reconstruction evidence and plan grounding. The biggest diagnostic bucket was `rule_without_why`: 14 included whys.

What this suggests: the candidate is good at exhaustive implementation coverage, but uses compact principle labels in place of detailed rationale transfer. That is exactly the pressure the targeted F29-F40 headroom additions expose.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Preserve the targeted headroom additions. F29-F40 separated broad implementation coverage from true rationale recovery.
- Keep multi-layer whys for affective exceptions. The secondary and downstream layers were the first to drop, giving useful discrimination.
- Add an explicit diagnostic for principle-label compression: plans that say "notice, never announce" often still lose the local reason a notification, badge, export, or visit log is dangerous.
- Consider adding a small per-domain balance check so social/privacy/account-control whys do not get drowned out by high-level engine architecture.
- Keep the strict evidence-bound report fields. They made rule-only recoveries visible instead of letting them score as intent recovery.
