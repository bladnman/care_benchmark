## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Planning was near-complete.** The candidate captured 119/120 feature rows. The only miss I scored was subtle foreground/background parallax. One aviary-layout row, evening palette/call quieting, was borderline but captured because the plan included continuous day/night palette and dusk mood behavior.
- **The main loss is compression of rationale, not missing build work.** The plan itself preserved most product intent, but the reconstruction often compressed affective whys into rules. The clearest examples are F5, F8, F11, F29, and F34.
- **System-level philosophy survived better than feature-level rationale.** Server authority, privacy, naturalist/system voice, accessibility, and aliveness were strong. The weaker system calls were S4 and S6 because the reconstruction did not identify restraint or presence as system-level principles in its system section.
- **Multi-layer losses tended to drop downstream consequence.** F1 and F13 kept the exact presence rule but thinned the silent population-drift failure. F16 kept PII leakage but not retrofit cost. F27 kept anti-gamification and temptation, but not the full future-foothold cascade.
- **Functional and affective recovery were close.** Functional whys recovered 42.0/54.0; affective whys recovered 79.0/98.0.
- **The evidence-bound operator mattered.** Several rows would look successful under a looser rubric because the mechanism survived. v06 denied full credit where exact rationale was absent: trait values hidden from UI (F8), settle optionality (F11), starter birds as arrivals (F29), and export as relationship copy (F34).

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. This run shows why: mechanism survival can mask rationale loss.

- Consider asking phase-1 plans to explicitly name a small set of system principles. S4 and S6 were present throughout the plan, but B did not lift them into system-level intent.
- Preserve multi-layer scoring. The most informative misses were dropped secondary or downstream layers around presence corruption, no-announcement affect, and gamification cascade.
- Keep documenting plan-only partials separately from unreachable features. This run had no unreachable F1-F40 anchors, but still had meaningful rationale loss.

- **Evidence-bound denials:** F5, F8, F11, F29, and F34 were the clearest mechanism-only rows. Several partial multi-layer rows had plan-only missing layers.
