## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional recovery was stronger because architecture evidence was explicit and repeated. Affective failures clustered where the plan named an implementation ban but the reconstruction did not preserve the relationship rationale, especially F23, F31, F33, and F37-F39.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys often landed as partial: primary mechanism survived, while secondary temptation and downstream product-collapse layers dropped. Examples include S2, S6, F9, F10, F14, F17, F18, F20, F27, F30, and F35.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The planner preserved philosophy and specifics; the reconstructor often narrowed philosophy to one surface. S1 became mostly first-frame aliveness, and S9 became mostly same-day accessibility shipping.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, privacy, audio, performance, and reduced-motion recovered well. Social sharing, visit logs, no-show-off rendering, and stable identity mostly survived as rules without their affective whys.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** v06 denied credit where B had only mechanisms: F5, F11, F19, F23, F31, F33, F37, F38, and F39. No ungrounded reconstruction cases were used for credit.

The failure shape suggests the plan was unusually complete, but the encoding from plan to reconstruction still favored build rules and architecture over subtle affective intent.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted headroom whys for social/privacy relationship boundaries. This run implemented the social mechanisms but often lost why they are not social-product surfaces.
- Preserve multi-layer scoring. It made clear that primary mechanisms survive more often than secondary temptation and downstream failure-mode layers.
- Add examples for system-level partials where B identifies a principle but narrows it to one surface; S1 and S9 were the main judgment-sensitive cases here.
- Keep evidence-bound reporting. Without it, rule-only rows like F31 and F38 would look deceptively close to recovery.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The frozen reconstruction reads as plan-derived, and the validity audit found no gold ID leakage or heading mirror. Same-context safeguard appears intact from available artifacts.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run only; no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Feature 7 and feature 52 were inclusive capture calls. Even marking both missed would leave planning at 98.3%, so the headline planning finding is stable.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The plan met the 3-feature bar for every S why; score loss came from B compression, not plan absence.
- **Confabulation cases.** No credited recovery depended on content that lacked plan grounding.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several plausible semantic recoveries were denied because they were mechanism-only rather than why-bearing.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** Nine included feature whys were marked rule-without-why, concentrated in social/notebook/identity and system-tone surfaces.
