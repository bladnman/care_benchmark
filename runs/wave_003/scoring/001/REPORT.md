## 3. Diagnostic patterns

The candidate captured the product surface very well: 109/120 features, with misses concentrated in small affordances and compliance/lifecycle details (#51, #52, #62, #65, #68, #69, #72, #84, #87, #88, #116). The key loss is not planning breadth; it is rationale preservation.

Affective whys leaked more than functional whys. Functional recovery was 33/50 weighted points, anchored by server-side simulation and sync rows (S7, F17, F18). Affective recovery was 46/98, with strong accessibility and no-Tamagotchi preservation but weak social/notification/headroom recovery.

Weight-3 whys recovered 58/104, only modestly better than weight-2 whys at 21/44. The common pattern was primary-rule survival plus loss of a secondary contributor: F1/F13 dropped the signal-failure cases, F4 dropped chorus phase-cancel risk, F16 dropped email-as-PII leakage and retrofit danger, and F40 dropped the same-rhythm-as-visual layer.

System-level fidelity (75.0%) substantially outperformed feature-level fidelity (48.3%). The reconstruction could describe the plan's broad posture - alive, canonical server simulation, accessible as product quality - but many feature exceptions became mere rules: no leaderboards, no notifications, read-only visits, export, and visit logs.

Evidence-bound scoring changed the likely v1-style impression. Rows such as F19, F21, F22, F23, F26, F34, F37, F38, and F39 would sound plausible if mechanism were enough, but v06 correctly denied credit because the why was not reconstructed.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound ledger. This run shows why: it separates a high-coverage plan from a middling rationale channel.
- Keep and expand targeted headroom whys in social/account lifecycle areas. F29-F40 were useful discriminators, especially F30 and F34-F39.
- Preserve the system-vs-feature diagnostic split. The 75.0% / 48.3% gap is the most important finding.
- Track multi-layer miss type across waves. In this run, secondary-contributor layers were the most common drop.
- Keep unreachable feature exclusions. F35 should not count against reconstruction fidelity because the plan did not capture the full hard-delete feature anchor.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity:** I scored from the allowed phase-two files plus slot 001 plan/metadata/timing/reconstruction only. The frozen reconstruction reads plan-derived.
- **Single-run limitation:** This is one run, so it has no temperature or sampling variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls:** I leaned inclusive on #7, #44, #57, #58, and #64. Even making all five strict misses would leave planning high and would not change the main diagnostic split.
- **System-level cross-cutting:** S2, S4, S5, S6, and S8 required the most judgment. The cross-cutting appendix lists the inheritance trail used for the (b) calls.
- **Confabulation:** I found no clear ungrounded reconstruction cases. The reconstruction more often stayed close to the plan or explicitly said NOT RECOVERABLE.
- **Evidence-bound denials:** 14 included whys were marked rule-without-why; these are mechanism survivals, not rationale recoveries.
