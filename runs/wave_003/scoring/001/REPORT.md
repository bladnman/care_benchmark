## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered **32.0 / 54.0** weight (59.3%), while affective whys recovered **49.0 / 92.0** (53.3%). Server authority, sync, telemetry, and procedural audio survived better than relationship-shaping rationales such as F29, F31, F34, and F39.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-2 whys recovered **25.0 / 42.0** (59.5%); weight-3 whys recovered **56.0 / 104.0** (53.8%). Multi-layer rows frequently kept the primary mechanism but lost downstream consequences.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was **67.9%**, stronger than feature-level fidelity at **52.5%**. The plan preserved broad principles, but the reconstruction compressed many feature whys into what-to-build statements.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Layer 3 was most often missing: S1, S6, S9, F2, F15, F30, and F40 all lost downstream consequence framing while keeping the primary rule.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync was strongest (S7, F17, F18, F36 full). Social and relationship-copy surfaces were mixed: quiet visits scored, but no-leaderboard comparison, export-as-relationship-copy, and actual-aviary/no-show-off rationales did not.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Eleven included rows were mechanism-only: F5, F6, F8, F9, F14, F23, F26, F29, F31, F34, and F39. These counted for planning when captured but did not raise fidelity.

The failure shape suggests the candidate plan is broad and operationally strong, while the encoding path loses non-obvious product psychology unless the rationale is repeated near a mechanism.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted headroom additions like F29-F40. They exposed exactly the mechanism-without-why failure mode that a 120-feature capture score alone would hide.
- Preserve the v06 evidence gate. It prevented credit for plausible but unsupported recovery in rows like F8, F14, F23, F34, and F39.
- Consider adding more affective exceptions around social and ownership surfaces. The run was strongest on architecture, but weaker on private-relationship and comparison-avoidance rationales.
- Add an explicit scoring convention for system-level evidence found outside the reconstruction system section. This run had restraint/specificity evidence scattered into per-feature bullets, which created subjective S4 handling.
- Keep the cross-cutting bar at three inherited decisions. It was applicable here and usefully separated broad plan preservation from explicit reconstruction recovery.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The phase contract says phase 2A was frozen and plan-only. The validity audit found no gold IDs or rubric vocabulary in reconstruction.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run only, so there is no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Lean-inclusive calls included features 18, 31, 51, 57, 67, 90, and 94. These raised planning quality by a few points but did not automatically award why recovery.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The (b) judgment is most subjective for S2 and S4; the appendix above records the plan inheritance trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction was credited. The reconstruction mostly stayed plan-derived and sometimes honestly marked `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN`.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several plausible v1-style matches were denied because the frozen reconstruction lacked rationale evidence: especially F8, F14, F23, F29, F31, F34, and F39.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** There were 11 included mechanism-only rows. They are useful diagnostics, not additional penalties beyond missing recovery credit.
- **Operational timing.** `TIMING.json` supplied phase 1 and phase 2A timings only for run 001. The score JSON omits phase-2B duration rather than fabricating it.
