## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Affective recovery was slightly higher overall (80.0/98.0) because major affective principles like S1, S2, S5, S9, F4, F9, F10, F24, and F25 survived. The misses were more subtle relationship rationales: F23 and F37 became rules, not whys.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 recovery (86.0/108.0) was a little lower than weight-2. Multi-layer rows often preserved primary mechanisms while dropping downstream consequences, especially F1/F13, F2, F12, F16, F30, and F35.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The plan preserved philosophy better than the reconstruction promoted it. System fidelity was 92.9%, but S3/S4 were plan-preserved and not reconstructed as named system intent. Feature fidelity was lower (77.4%) because feature-level downstream rationale compressed away.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Audio, server-side sync, accessibility, and rendering loaded well. Social/privacy-adjacent sharing rationales were weaker: F23 and F37 lost the private-relationship/comparison rationale even though the no-leaderboard and per-invite rules survived.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** F19, F23, and F37 would look acceptable under a rule-only read, but v06 denies credit because the why itself was absent. That is the main useful bite of this operator in this run.

What the failure shape suggests: the candidate plan is excellent at preserving buildable product constraints, while reconstruction tends to compress poetic or relationship-preserving consequences into operational rules.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound ledger; it cleanly separated feature capture from why recovery.

- Consider asking the reconstructor to produce a short final principles-recovered-from-plan pass after the per-feature pass. S3 and S4 were visible in the plan, but not lifted into system-level intent.
- Preserve the strict system-level cross-cutting bar. In this run the bar was not the problem; the PLAN inherited S3/S4 across multiple places, while the frozen reconstruction omitted those principles.
