## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional:** functional whys recovered much better (48.0/54.0) than affective whys (66.0/98.0). The server-side tick, presence accounting, no-LWW sync, and telemetry boundary were strong; the social/relationship rationales behind F23, F37, F38, and F39 leaked.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2:** weight-3 whys recovered at 81.5%, much higher than weight-2 at 59.1%. The plan's most load-bearing architectural constraints were easier to reconstruct than smaller exception rationales.
- **System vs feature:** system-level fidelity (85.7%) exceeded feature-level fidelity (72.6%). The reconstruction identified the big architecture and philosophy but frequently compressed feature-level whys into rules.
- **Multi-layer pattern:** primary mechanisms usually survived; secondary affective explanations and downstream relationship consequences were the common missing layers.
- **Subdomain pattern:** simulation, sync, accessibility, and telemetry scored well. Social_optional and several restraint/specificity exceptions were weaker.
- **Evidence-bound effect:** F6, F8, F19, F23, F29, F37, F38, and F39 were counted as rule-without-why or none because the frozen reconstruction did not recover the rationale.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add or sharpen feature-level whys around social sharing, visit logs, and private-relationship exposure; this run implemented the rules while losing much of the why.
- Keep the evidence-bound operator and rule-without-why count; they created useful separation between planning coverage and rationale preservation.
- Make S3 and S4 easier to audit with more concrete layer hooks, since specificity/restraint can be preserved in implementation without being named by the reconstructor.
- Retain multi-layer scoring for high-weight whys; it captured partial but incomplete preservation in F14, F30, F31, F35, and F40.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity:** I treated the frozen reconstruction as immutable and did not edit it. The supplied phase setup says phase 2A read only the plan.
- **Single-run limitation:** one candidate and one reconstruction provide no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls:** I leaned inclusive on the glossary, starter-bird adoption flow, and settle undo. This kept all F1-F40 anchors reachable.
- **System cross-cutting:** S3 and S4 were subjective; I awarded partial because the plan preserved the principles but the reconstruction did not name them directly.
- **Confabulation:** no material ungrounded reconstruction cases were found. The reconstructor often used NOT RECOVERABLE rather than inventing rationales.
- **Timing:** TIMING.json supplied phase1 and phase2a only for run 001; no phase2b timing was present.
