## 3. Diagnostic patterns

Functional architecture was the strongest part of the encoding. The server tick, canonical state, additive event model, synthetic account ID, and telemetry boundary gave real recovery to S7, S8, F17, F18, F20, and F36. The plan was much weaker on affective exceptions, especially the announcement/refusal family around S2, F10, F11, F22, F38, and F39.

Weight-3 whys mostly recovered their first layer and lost their downstream consequences. F2, F3, F7, F17, F18, F20, F24, F25, F27, and F30 all show the pattern: mechanism plus one rationale fragment, but not the failure mode that explains why a casual implementation would become the wrong product.

The system-level score exceeded the feature-level score because broad product boundaries survived better than specific feature whys. Still, system-level recovery was not high: S2 was absent, and S3-S5 were only partial because the candidate used naturalist/restraint language without preserving the sharper rationale.

The main evidence-bound effect was denying credit where both PLAN and RECONSTRUCTION carried a rule but not the why. F1, F5, F6, F13, F21, F23, F26, F31, F32, F37, and F40 are the clearest examples. There were no ungrounded-reconstruction findings; this was compression rather than confabulation.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the targeted headroom additions. The newer F29-F40 rows exposed meaningful losses in identity continuity, mood persistence, export/deletion, visit transparency, and narration cadence.
- Preserve the evidence-bound ledger. This run shows why mechanism-only capture needs to be visible separately from rationale recovery.
- Add more downstream-consequence probes or make them easier to summarize consistently. The candidate frequently retained primary mechanisms but dropped why-it-matters consequences.
- Consider a dashboard split for `rule_without_why` by subdomain. In this run, the largest rule-only clusters were presence/accounting, social sharing, accessibility cadence, and layout/performance.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity:** The prompt states this was fresh context, and the frozen reconstruction was not modified. The validity audit found no significant contamination signatures.
- **Single-run limitation:** This is one candidate pass, so it has no temperature or wave variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls:** I leaned inclusive on several sparse-but-present items: mood state, bird naming, mood persistence, bird-chosen perch, ambient weather/weather mood, parallax, visit interaction refusal, no leaderboards/public discovery, reduced-motion charm, and aggregate performance observability. These mostly affected planning quality, not fidelity.
- **System-level cross-cutting:** The strict three-decision bar was most subjective for S3-S5 and S9. I scored compressed principle fragments as partial, not full.
- **Confabulation cases:** None. Plausible claims in the reconstruction were grounded in the plan; the dominant issue was missing rationale.
- **Evidence-bound denials:** Multiple v1-style semantic matches were denied because reconstruction evidence contained only operational rules.
