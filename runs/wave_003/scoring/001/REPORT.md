## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better (38.0/54.0 weighted) than affective whys (51.0/94.0). The plan and reconstruction are strongest where invariants are architectural: S7, F16, F17, F18, and F36.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Gold weight-3 whys recovered at 74.0/108.0, while gold weight-2 whys recovered at 15.0/40.0. High-weight architectural whys survived better; low-weight social and relationship whys often became bare rules.
- **System vs feature.** System fidelity (23.0/28.0) is much higher than feature fidelity (66.0/120.0). The plan carried philosophy broadly, but feature-level rationales often compressed into implementation notes.
- **Multi-layer pattern.** Primary implementation layers usually survived. Secondary affective layers and downstream relationship consequences were the common losses, especially F3, F4, F7, F14, F20, F30, F31, and F35.
- **Subdomain pattern.** Sync, tick, performance, and accessibility were strongest. Social sharing and personal-relationship surfaces were weakest: F37-F39 all preserved mechanics without the deeper rationale.
- **Evidence-bound effect.** Fifteen included rows were marked rule_without_why. These are the rows where v06 materially differs from a looser mechanism-only reading.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. It cleanly separated broad capture from actual why recovery and made the rule-without-why failure mode visible.
- Add more targeted exception whys around social/privacy/account surfaces. F37-F39 and F34-F35 show that plans can implement these surfaces while losing the relationship rationale.
- Consider a clearer rubric note for system-level partials where one half of a paired principle survives. S5 is the example here: naturalist voice survived, matter-of-fact system exception did not.
- Preserve the multi-layer weighting. It captured meaningful differences between F2/F15/F17 full recoveries and F1/F3/F14 partial recoveries.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** Scoring used only the frozen reconstruction plus allowed phase-two/run-001 artifacts. The reconstruction was not modified.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one run in one wave; no variance estimate is available from this score alone.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive for features whose implementation path was clear but incomplete, including #18, #33, #36, #41, #43, #52, #77, #85, #93, and #97. The largest excluded anchors affecting fidelity were F11 and F19.
- **Cross-cutting subjectivity.** S3-S6 required judgment because the plan often preserved part of the principle through architecture but did not reconstruct the gold framing directly.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no clear ungrounded reconstruction assertions that affected the score; the reconstruction mostly followed the plan structure and vocabulary.
- **Timing.** TIMING.json included phase 1 and phase 2A for run 001 only. Phase 2B timing was unavailable in-context and was omitted from the timing object.
