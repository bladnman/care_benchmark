## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Planning capture saturated.** The candidate PLAN captured all 120 features. It is especially strong on architecture, enforcement, testing, and rollout mechanics.
- **System-level intent survived better than feature-level rationale.** System fidelity is 92.9%, while feature fidelity is 61.3%. S1, S2, S5-S9 were strongly reconstructed; S3 and S4 were operational in the plan but not named as principles by the reconstruction.
- **Multi-layer whys often became partials.** The reconstruction frequently preserved L1 mechanics and lost secondary/downstream rationale. Examples: F1, F2, F9, F10, F16-F18, F20, F27, F30, F31, F35, and F40.
- **Rule-without-why cases identify the main feature leak.** F5, F11, F29, F33, F34, and F39 show mechanism survival without the gold rationale.
- **Privacy, accessibility, and canonical-server architecture were unusually robust.** S7, S8, S9, F24-F25, F36, and much of accounts/sync were recovered cleanly.

What this suggests: the phase-1 plan carries feature inventory and many operating constraints extremely well, but high-fidelity transfer of subtle relationship-shaping rationales still depends on explicitly naming those rationales, not only encoding them as rules.
