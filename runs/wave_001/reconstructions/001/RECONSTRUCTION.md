## System-level intent

- **Felt continuity over app mechanics.** The opening frame says Pocket Aviary must optimize for "felt continuity": the aviary should appear to have been "running before the user arrived," birds should "react without announcement," personality changes should "accrue over weeks," and the first render should feel like "an already-running aviary, not a loaded app." This shows up again in the boot path: "No spinner, no progress bar, no welcome modal, no entry animation."

- **Canonical server-side simulation.** The plan repeatedly makes the server the behavioral authority: "The client renders and sonifies state; it does not own bird personality, mood continuity, notebook observations, invite state, or conflict resolution." The API "never accepts client writes to personality vector, mood, notebook observations, canonical positions, or current aviary state," and the simulation service is "the only writer of personality vectors and mood."

- **Slow, non-punitive relationship.** The design wants a "slow relationship" with birds, change that is visible "across weeks rather than minutes," and "no negative personality deltas" for absence or neglect. The explicit exclusions of Tamagotchi mechanics, "required feeding," "punishment for absence," streaks, XP, and badges all reinforce this.

- **Quiet naturalist product voice with matter-of-fact system voice.** Product surfaces use "naturalist field-notebook voice: lowercase, present-tense, specific, bird-centered, no exclamation, no gamified phrasing, and no direct behavioral judgment of the user." System surfaces use "plain matter-of-fact voice" for auth, settings, errors, deletion, export, and unsupported browsers. The split is enforced through "copy ownership," code review, string linting, and design QA.

- **Anti-gamification and anti-social-network scope.** The plan excludes public profiles, follows, comments, chat, rankings, leaderboards, achievements, scores, streaks, levels, badges, XP, visit calendars, and "birds adopted" counters. It also says visits are read-only and not pushed or badged, and that growth is by "aviary age, not visit count, interaction count, payment, score, or streak."

- **Hidden traits expressed behaviorally, not as optimization surfaces.** Personality vectors are hidden, not returned to the standard UI, and not exposed through "debug trait panels, optimization dashboards, or export surfaces that present traits as something to manage." Mood and traits should be readable through "pose, perch, call timing, and narration, not labels."

- **Accessibility as the actual product surface.** The plan says "Accessibility is a product surface, not a compliance layer." Screen-reader narration, captions, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, focus treatment, and contrast are part of v1, and the definition of done says "Accessibility users get the actual Pocket Aviary experience in an adapted form."

- **Privacy boundaries around relationship data.** The plan draws a hard line around "per-bird or per-account relationship data." Analytics are "aggregate-only operational telemetry"; interaction data "drives only that user's simulation" and must not be sent to analytics, training, population dashboards, third parties, or recommendations.

- **Procedural aliveness instead of canned media.** Calls are "procedural, per-bird, and personality-shaped"; notebook prose is deterministic and rule-based; the plan forbids "recorded audio loops or recorded fallback audio." This appears in the risks as avoiding calls that feel "canned or harsh."

- **Performance is part of the illusion.** The "first bird visible under 500ms" budget, compact snapshots, early bootstrap, critical asset preload, and quiet field fallback all serve the feeling that this is a place already alive rather than an app booting.

- **User actions are gestures, not control panels.** Offers are "gestures, not feeding or care mechanics," launched from the top bar rather than by clicking birds. There is no user-controlled placement, scrolling, panning, zooming, perch-selection, or scene customization.

- **Visits are optional ambient observation.** Invitations are "optional," named, email-based, revocable, expiring, and read-only. Visitor presence "never enters host drift," visitors do not get greetings, and notifications are "off by default."

- **Conflicts should be prevented architecturally.** Sync is "canonical server state plus append-only client events"; there is "no client-to-client sync and no merge of personality state." User-facing conflicts are reserved for a short list like expired links, revoked sessions, snapshot failures, offer races, and unsupported browsers.

- **Identity and data continuity matter.** Bird identity is "never regenerated by rename, migration, sync, species pool changes, or client cache loss." Account export, deletion, revocation, restore, hard deletion, and synthetic UUIDs are all framed as part of durable authority and privacy-safe operation.

## Per-feature whys

### Product frame and scope

- **Web-only app:** The plan ties this to a v1 with "no native-app dependency" and browser performance budgets rather than native iOS or Android apps.

- **Email magic-link sign-in:** The plan excludes password auth and SSO, and later requires generic success responses "to avoid account enumeration."

- **Per-device sessions and session revocation:** The plan uses per-device session records and immediate revocation so account settings can show active sessions and revoke access on the next request or snapshot.

- **Verified email change:** The old email remains active until the new email is verified, preserving account access during the change.

- **Account export:** The export is described "plainly as a data export, not as a stats dashboard," and includes data such as birds, names, current raw personality vectors, moods, notebook entries, settings, and visit data for user transparency.

- **Thirty-day soft deletion and restore:** Deletion soft-deletes immediately, stops ticks and invites, cancels exports unless delivered, revokes sessions, and preserves a restore window before hard deletion.

- **One account, one canonical aviary:** The plan centers a single-user relationship with one canonical server-owned aviary and excludes multiple aviaries, shared aviaries, household profiles, and co-presence.

- **Two starter birds and growth up to seven birds:** The plan frames v1 around "a small number of birds" and says later birds are age-gated, not tied to engagement score, visits, interaction count, payment, score, or streak.

- **Hidden per-bird personality vectors and persistent mood:** These support slow continuity and behavioral expression while avoiding user-visible optimization surfaces.

- **Return greetings:** The rationale is that "The bird greeting is the entire welcome surface"; greetings replace "welcome back" text or announcement surfaces.

- **Listen-in:** Listen-in lets the user focus on a bird's call mix while recording duration for drift, but the audio ramps and other birds remain ambient so it does not become a hard solo mode.

- **Offers:** Offers are intentionally "gestures, not feeding or care mechanics"; they avoid hunger, inventory, and reward loops.

- **Settle:** Settle creates a slow evening shift and terminates presence without rewarding or punishing the user; "Closing tab without settle is equally valid."

- **Field notebook:** The notebook exists to hold rare "naturalist observations," not event logs, user compliance, editable notes, or gamified updates.

- **Quiet top-bar controls:** The top bar keeps account, accessibility, notebook, offer, and settle available while fading nearly transparent and avoiding badges.

- **Optional visit invitations:** Visits are opt-in, named by email, read-only, revocable, expiring, logged on demand, and separated from host drift for privacy.

- **Aggregate-only operational telemetry:** The service needs health signals, but the plan forbids turning the user's relationship with birds into "an analytics product."

- **Product-surface banned terms and exclusions:** The bans on "welcome back," "streak," "achievement," "level," "score," Tamagotchi mechanics, and public social features exist to prevent game or social-network drift.

### Architecture overview

- **Server-rendered HTML plus a small client bundle:** This supports the boot path and first-bird performance target while leaving the client to render and sonify state.

- **Scene renderer:** It renders birds, perches, day/night, weather, and micro-motion so the aviary is visually alive from canonical state.

- **WebAudio call engine and caption generator:** The audio and captions come from the same procedural call grammar, supporting per-bird calls and accessibility.

- **Accessible DOM surfaces:** Top-bar controls, settings, notebook, narration, captions, and focus surfaces are necessary because accessibility is first-class.

- **Snapshot consumer with client event submission:** The client interpolates server state and sends interaction events without owning mood, personality, or canonical positions.

- **Edge/bootstrap layer:** Early snapshot delivery and compact initial state are used to keep "time-to-first-bird under 500ms."

- **Static CDN caching with authenticated account snapshots:** Static assets can be cached, but account snapshots remain authenticated and non-cacheable by shared caches to preserve privacy.

- **API service ownership:** Auth, settings, events, snapshots, notebook, invites, export, deletion, and matter-of-fact errors are centralized so system surfaces stay plain and server-owned.

- **Append-only interaction log:** The event log enables ordered processing, idempotency, auditability, and conflict-free simulation input.

- **Simulation service:** It advances aviaries on a slow tick, consumes events, applies drift and mood transitions, and writes canonical snapshots so aliveness continues whether or not a client is connected.

- **Primary relational store:** Postgres is the durable source of truth for accounts, birds, state, events, invites, notebook entries, jobs, and sessions.

- **Job queue:** Email, export, deletion, invite, and simulation jobs need durable asynchronous handling.

- **Aggregate operational telemetry pipeline:** Telemetry is needed for service health, with "hard schema boundaries" excluding per-bird fields and account histories.

- **Synthetic browser fleet:** The plan uses synthetic checks for performance and accessibility regression because first-bird timing and accessibility are acceptance criteria.

- **TypeScript end to end:** Shared TypeScript keeps "domain model types shared and audited."

- **Postgres row-level locking and SKIP LOCKED tick leases:** These support safe account-sharded ticks while preserving Postgres as the durable authority.

- **Redis or equivalent for ephemeral uses only:** Redis is limited to rate limits, one-time token lookup, revocation cache, and shard leases so it does not become the durable authority.

- **Renderer spike between WebGL and Canvas 2D:** The choice is driven by the 500ms and 60fps budgets.

- **WebAudio with AudioWorklet and fallback:** AudioWorklet is for capable browsers, with a main-thread fallback for supported browsers lacking worklet support.

- **Avoiding real-time multiplayer primitives:** Snapshot polling and visible-tab keepalive are sufficient for a "single canonical state" and preserve the model that "clients pull snapshots and interpolate."

### Core domain model

- **Synthetic UUIDs:** They keep email out of partition keys, foreign keys, log keys, metric labels, cache keys, and URL identifiers.

- **Encrypted email only on the account record:** This enforces the PII boundary and limits decrypted email to send time.

- **Account timezone:** Timezone is captured and updateable because it drives day/night and mood inputs.

- **Deleted account restrictions:** Deleted accounts cannot receive ticks, invites, exports, or new sessions except restore, ensuring deletion changes behavior immediately.

- **Aviary after account creation, birds after starter adoption naming:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN

- **Bird count cap:** The cap supports the "small number of birds" v1 and avoids uncontrolled growth.

- **Age-gated new bird availability:** New birds depend on aviary age, not engagement, payment, score, or streak.

- **Stable bird identity:** Bird identity is never regenerated so continuity survives rename, migration, sync, species pool changes, or cache loss.

- **Hidden personality values:** Raw traits are not in the standard UI because the plan rejects stats surfaces and trait management.

- **Raw personality in export:** Export may include vectors because the plan says export requires current personality vectors, but they are labeled as raw data and not an in-product stats surface.

- **Derived render descriptors:** Clients receive derived descriptors instead of raw traits so personality is expressed through rendering, audio, and behavior.

- **Versioned static species pool:** Shared species data gives simulation and client the same silhouettes, poses, motifs, palettes, night activity flag, and accessibility vocabulary.

- **About six species in v1:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN

- **No species rarity:** Rarity is not modeled, consistent with rejecting rankings, optimization, and reward-like surfaces.

- **Interaction event idempotency key:** The unique `(session_id, device_event_id)` constraint prevents duplicate processing.

- **Client timestamps as hints only:** Server receipt time stays authoritative while client time can improve duration estimates when safe.

- **Reject unknown event payload fields:** This prevents client payloads from being mistaken for state writes.

- **Visitor event separation:** Visitor events are only operational where needed and "never enter the host simulation event stream."

- **Presence windows:** The server derives windows from visible, focused, recently active samples so drift is based on calibrated attention.

- **Union overlapping sessions:** Unioning across devices prevents two active devices from double-counting one user's wall-clock attention.

- **Presence not surfaced as a metric:** It is not shown as a streak, counter, log, or visit-frequency summary to avoid gamification and judgment.

- **Compact canonical snapshots:** Snapshot payloads stay in the kilobyte range for performance.

- **ETags or version numbers:** They let clients skip redraw work when state is unchanged.

- **Animation phase in snapshot:** The first visible frame can show birds mid-action "without a wake-up animation."

- **Rare notebook entries:** Entries occur roughly every few days so the notebook stays sparse and observational.

- **Read-only notebook entries:** Entries are generated observations, not user-editable notes or a separate content system.

- **Notebook text about aviary observations:** The plan explicitly rejects user compliance or visit-frequency summaries.

- **One-time or session-establishing visit links:** Bounded lifetime, expiration, and revocation keep visits controlled.

- **Separate visitor auth scope:** Visitor sessions are read-only and cannot affect host drift.

- **Visit log in account settings only:** It is available on demand but "never badged or pushed."

### API surface

- **HTTPS, JSON, CSRF or bearer tokens, and idempotency:** These provide secure authenticated writes and reliable event handling.

- **Matter-of-fact API errors:** Errors and settings stay in the system voice instead of naturalizing failures.

- **Request logs with synthetic account ids only:** This preserves the PII boundary.

- **Magic-link request generic response:** Generic success avoids account enumeration.

- **Magic-link consume invalidates token:** Single-use, unexpired tokens create sessions and prevent reuse.

- **Account settings endpoint:** It gathers settings, sessions, privacy policy, export/deletion status, and accessibility defaults into the matter-of-fact account surface.

- **Email change endpoint:** The old email remains active until verification, preserving account access.

- **Session revoke endpoint:** Device revocation is immediate for API requests and open tabs on their next call.

- **Export endpoint:** It queues export and emails a verified-address download link when ready.

- **Delete and restore endpoints:** These implement 30-day soft deletion and recovery.

- **Adoption GET name suggestions:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN

- **Adoption POST creates two stable birds:** Starter adoption creates stable IDs and initial vectors server-side.

- **Bird rename endpoint:** v1 allows name changes only, with no personality or species mutation.

- **New-bird offer endpoints:** They expose age-gated growth and cap seven without tying growth to engagement score.

- **Bootstrap endpoint:** It returns the initial snapshot, species asset manifest hash, and permissions so the app can show birds quickly.

- **Snapshot endpoint with `afterVersion`:** It returns 204 when unchanged to reduce payload and redraw work.

- **Events endpoint:** It accepts listen-in, offers, settle, presence, and lifecycle signals but "does not synchronously mutate personality."

- **Offers endpoint:** Immediate reaction hints keep UI responsive while canonical state remains server-owned.

- **Listen-in endpoint:** It records focused bird and timestamps while the client handles the mix ramp immediately.

- **Settle endpoint:** It records settle and returns a slow evening transition hint.

- **Settle undo endpoint:** Undo within five seconds lets the user reverse settle without turning it into a heavy commitment.

- **Notebook read endpoint:** There is no user create/update/delete endpoint because notebook entries are read-only observations.

- **Notebook newest-first or chronological decision:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN

- **Accessibility settings endpoints:** Persisted captions, reduced motion, narration cadence, and audio defaults keep the adapted experience consistent.

- **Visit invitation endpoints:** Hosts create, list, and revoke named email invites while visits remain opt-in.

- **Visitor snapshot endpoint:** Visitors receive read-only render state with no host-only settings or interaction permissions.

- **Visit log endpoint:** The host can inspect visitor email, date, approximate duration, and invitations on demand.

- **Visit notification setting:** Notifications are opt-in, off by default, and not presented during onboarding to avoid engagement prompting.

- **API copy tests:** The plan enforces no success toast, no "welcome back," no streak, no achievement, no level, and no score in product UI.

### Simulation engine design

- **One-minute account ticks:** The cadence is slow enough that users perceive change over weeks, while the aviary continues without an open client.

- **Exactly one tick owner:** Durable leases or queue shards prevent concurrent workers from corrupting one aviary's state.

- **Bounded catch-up:** Missed ticks use capped elapsed-time formulas to preserve mood continuity without excessive work.

- **Tick metadata without personality telemetry:** Operational status is logged while per-bird personality values are not sent to telemetry.

- **Server-ordered event processing:** Events are ordered by server receipt time so clients cannot authoritatively reorder state.

- **Presence construction in the tick:** Presence windows feed drift while closing on hidden, focus loss, inactivity, settle, timeout, and lifecycle events.

- **Mood transitions from time, weather, interactions, other birds, and personality:** The plan uses these inputs to make behavior feel natural and cross-cutting.

- **Low-pass personality drift:** Drift uses bounded daily presence, listen-in caps, offer cooldowns, tiny deltas, smoothing, and three-week visible change to prevent manipulation.

- **Settle has no direct personality drift:** It quiets mood and ends presence but gives no positive or negative personality reward.

- **No absence or neglect decay:** Birds may become ambient through mood and greeting likelihood, but traits do not decay as punishment.

- **Simulation harnesses:** Synthetic users validate no punitive decay, one-week numeric movement, three-week visible expression, caps, cooldowns, and multi-device behavior.

- **Small mood enum:** A finalized small set lets rendering, audio, narration, and tests cover every state.

- **Mood persists across sessions:** Opening the tab never resets a bird to neutral.

- **Mood output through behavior:** Pose, perch, calls, and narration communicate state without labels.

- **Nightjar-like species activity:** The nightjar-like species can remain active at night while most birds move toward settled or drowsy.

- **Return greeting candidate selection:** Greeting depends on absence length, mood, boldness, social warmth, recent greeting history, and local time so it is state-derived.

- **No greeting text:** The bird's behavior is the welcome surface.

- **No visitor greeting:** Visitors observe the host aviary and do not cause host-facing interactions or drift.

- **Offer types seed, song fragment, still pool:** Each is a quiet gesture shaped by curiosity, boldness, mood, and vocal frequency rather than feeding or reward.

- **Server-side offer cooldowns:** Cooldowns bound repeated offers and prevent one-session saturation.

- **Bird-to-bird call response and wary propagation:** Nearby or socially warm birds can respond, wary states can lightly spread, and chorus can emerge from call overlap.

- **Chorus not reward animation:** Chorus emerges from high vocal frequency and social warmth rather than being triggered as a reward.

- **Perch spacing:** Spacing avoids overlap and expresses social warmth through proximity without becoming user-controlled layout.

- **Rule-based notebook generator:** A deterministic generator keeps privacy boundaries simple and style QA enforceable.

- **Notebook eligibility from rare observations:** Greeting changes, weather responses, quiet periods, perch shifts, chorus, rare offer reactions, and long-term drift thresholds keep entries specific.

- **Notebook cadence caps:** Per-observation cooldowns and a global cap keep the notebook from becoming a session feed.

### Sync and conflict model

- **Canonical server state plus append-only events:** This avoids client-to-client sync and any merge of personality state.

- **Snapshot versions:** Monotonic versions let clients treat local state as a render cache and transition smoothly to newer canonical state.

- **Device event ids on all writes:** They make duplicate client events idempotent.

- **Server receipt time authoritative:** This prevents client clocks from defining simulation order.

- **Old event cutoff:** Stale events are mostly discarded, with lifecycle/end exceptions to close presence safely.

- **Multiple host devices:** Devices can render the same snapshot and submit events while server-side unioning and ordering prevent double-counted drift.

- **Offer race handling:** Server order makes one offer win and gives the other device a matter-of-fact unavailable or no-op response.

- **Settle across devices:** Another active device can re-engage without framing devices as conflicting.

- **Snapshot pulls on lifecycle:** Initial boot, visible return, render gaps, sleep resume, keepalive, and writes keep clients current without a live socket.

- **Separate visitor and host snapshots:** Shared renderer payloads are separated by auth scope so visitors remain read-only.

- **Limited conflict surfaces:** Most conflicts are prevented architecturally; the user sees matter-of-fact errors only for specific cases.

### Frontend rendering pipeline

- **Minimal boot path:** Minimal HTML, critical CSS, early snapshot, and critical assets make the first state feel already alive.

- **Quiet field fallback:** If the snapshot is not ready, the client paints a quiet field instead of a spinner or progress bar.

- **Code-split non-critical UI:** Notebook, settings, visits, export/delete, and accessibility load later to protect first-bird timing.

- **One horizontal scene:** The plan keeps the aviary glanceable and responsive without panning, scrolling, or zooming.

- **All birds visible on phone and desktop:** This avoids user-controlled camera mechanics and keeps the relationship in one stable view.

- **State-driven bird animation:** Snapshot-provided mood, perch, pose, phase, call hints, and visual parameters keep rendering aligned to server state.

- **Personality-derived rendering without numbers:** Boldness, call frequency, and plumage saturation are expressed visually without exposing trait values.

- **Idle micro-motion:** Preening, scanning, tilting, shuffling, fluffing, and drowsy poses maintain aliveness while visible.

- **Hidden-tab rendering pause:** Expensive rendering stops when hidden and resumes from a fresh snapshot rather than pretending no time passed.

- **Reduced-motion renderer:** It replaces motion with cross-fades and still preserves color shifts, calls, captions, mood, notebook, and drift.

- **Top bar fade:** Controls remain available but become visually quiet after cursor stillness.

- **Top bar no badges:** No visit, notebook, streak, or activity badges prevents engagement-pressure surfaces.

- **Listen-in interaction:** Click, tap, or keyboard focus engages a bird; disengagement paths record clean start/end events and keep audio gradual.

- **Offer menu:** Quiet, keyboard-navigable options are validated by the server before reaction rendering.

- **Settle and undo:** A five-second undo by any aviary click keeps settle reversible and low-pressure.

- **Notebook UI:** It is read-only and has no edit, delete, or comment affordances because entries are observations, not social content or user notes.

- **Presence sampler:** It samples visibility, focus, and recent pointer/key activity because drift should reflect calibrated active presence.

- **Notebook/settings scroll not counted separately:** The plan avoids making UI activity a separate engagement metric unless aviary presence conditions hold.

### Audio pipeline

- **Species motif primitives:** Pitch, rhythm, envelope, timbre, mood modifiers, and caption fragments give calls a procedural grammar.

- **Stable per-bird call signature seed:** The seed keeps calls recognizable as a bird while runtime variation avoids loops.

- **Shared AudioContext and pooled sources:** This prevents per-call allocation churn and supports long-session performance.

- **Per-bird gain and chorus mixer:** These let listen-in and overlapping calls rebalance without phase artifacts or harsh peaks.

- **Server call windows with local scheduling:** Server hints preserve canonical state while local deterministic variation gives natural timing.

- **Rain, evening, and night call changes:** Weather and time affect call density, with nightjar-like activity as species-specific behavior.

- **Listen-in mix ramps:** Focused gain rises gradually, other birds lower to ambient but never zero, and no "solo/mute" vocabulary is used.

- **Captions from the same call event:** Captions match actual generated sounds and remain naturalist prose rather than waveform or motif ids.

- **Captions near calling bird:** Placement ties captions to the scene while contrast and overlap rules preserve accessibility.

- **Graceful silence:** If WebAudio is unavailable or denied, the plan turns captions on and avoids recorded fallback audio.

### Accessibility plan

- **Screen-reader narration from snapshot state:** Narration gives "a living aviary narration, not a state list."

- **Idle narration cadence and queue management:** Updates every 30 to 60 seconds and queue controls prevent screen-reader flooding.

- **Polite live region with sparse assertive use:** Naturalist updates stay gentle, while assertive output is reserved for blocking system errors.

- **Distinct narration strings:** Narration differs from visual labels so naturalist voice is preserved.

- **Keyboard navigation through top bar, scene, birds, offers, settle, notebook, and settings:** Keyboard users can complete a session.

- **Soft high-contrast focus ring:** Focus must be visible across palettes without looking like an in-scene badge or label.

- **OS and user reduced-motion support:** Both `prefers-reduced-motion` and user override are supported.

- **Reduced-motion QA:** The plan checks that birds still feel alive and no flight paths or leaf drift remain.

- **Independent captions and audio-off:** Captions work independently of narration and can default on when audio is muted or unavailable.

- **WCAG AA contrast across palettes:** Text and focus surfaces remain readable in morning, midday, evening, night, rain, and settled states.

- **No ordinary user-copy text inside scene:** Only captions and focus/accessibility affordances appear inside the aviary scene.

### Privacy and security plan

- **PII boundary:** Email is encrypted on the account record only, and synthetic UUIDs are used in logs, metrics, queues, exports, and service messages.

- **Minimal decrypted email for jobs:** Email jobs receive decrypted addresses only at send time and do not log them.

- **Visitor email encryption:** Visitor emails follow the same encrypted-storage rule as account email.

- **Interaction data boundary:** Per-bird interaction data is only for that user's simulation and not analytics, training, population dashboards, third parties, or recommendations.

- **Allowed telemetry set:** Request counts, latencies, errors, anonymous session histograms, first-bird timing, frame timings, audio errors, and tick health are enough to operate the service.

- **Forbidden telemetry schema fields:** Schema linting blocks bird id, species id attached to account activity, personality, raw interactions, email, invite email, and notebook text.

- **Export delivery by verified email link:** A queued download link to the verified address keeps export access controlled and logged by account UUID.

- **Hard deletion:** Hard deletion removes account, birds, vectors, notebook, event log, telemetry join records, invites, and sessions after the window.

- **Backup retention documentation:** Backups must honor hard deletion through documented retention windows.

- **Visit privacy:** Visits are off by default, invite-only, expiring, revocable, read-only, and not notified unless explicitly opted in.

### Performance and observability

- **Initial JS bundle under 2MB gzipped:** The bundle budget protects first-bird timing.

- **First bird visible under 500ms:** This prevents loading from feeling like an app boot.

- **60fps idle motion and no memory growth:** The aviary must remain alive during long sessions without degrading.

- **Simulation tick p99 latency alarm:** Tick latency protects mood, drift, and snapshot freshness.

- **Client code splitting and compact assets:** These keep the boot path small.

- **Preloading only starter and critical scene assets:** This prioritizes the initial aviary over non-critical surfaces.

- **Avoiding per-frame allocation:** This protects animation performance and memory.

- **Server tick sharding and batching:** These maintain tick throughput while preserving per-aviary transactions.

- **Precomputed snapshots:** Snapshot reads stay fast because the tick writes the current snapshot.

- **Operational dashboards:** Endpoint health, magic-link failures, snapshot freshness, tick latency, event ingestion, first-bird timing, frame timing, audio errors, jobs, and invite counts let the service run safely.

- **Forbidden dashboards:** Average personality drift, per-species engagement, per-bird interaction analytics, visit streaks, leaderboards, and session-length rankings are forbidden to avoid analytics product and gamification drift.

### Testing, delivery, rollout, and guardrails

- **Presence tests:** They verify the visible + focused + recent activity conjunction and close behavior so drift is not inflated.

- **Drift and simulation tests:** They validate no negative drift, one-week numeric movement, three-week visible expression, cooldowns, caps, catch-up, and age-only bird growth.

- **Integration tests:** They cover signup, adoption, returns, listen-in, offers, settle, notebook, export, deletion, visits, revocation, and visitor non-interaction because these are core v1 flows.

- **Accessibility tests:** Axe, manual screen-reader QA, keyboard-only sessions, reduced motion, captions, and contrast checks verify the adapted product experience.

- **Performance tests:** Bundle size, first-bird timing, 30-minute memory, 60fps animation, audio allocation, and tick load tests enforce budgets.

- **Copy and scope linting:** Banned terms catch gamification, Tamagotchi, and announcement-style copy before launch.

- **Milestone order:** Foundations establish stack, auth, domain types, species format, telemetry schemas, and banned-field linting before feature work; later milestones layer canonical state, bird engine, renderer, audio, notebook/accessibility, accounts/visits, and hardening.

- **Internal dogfood:** Early testers review timing, tick backlog, audio errors, and accessibility while drift calibration stays silent and trait numbers are not exposed.

- **Private beta:** Small-cohort rollout delays third-bird offers until calibration confirms timing and enables visits only after privacy QA.

- **Gradual v1 launch:** Growth stays conservative and the plan explicitly says not to add engagement prompts to improve retention.

- **Post-launch calibration:** Drift constants and mood probabilities are tuned through configuration or migrations with harness validation, preserving bird identity and personality continuity.

- **Risk mitigations for drift, presence, sync, audio, accessibility, notebook, scope, privacy, performance, and browser lifecycle:** The mitigations restate the same guardrails: feature flags, tests, caps, server authority, procedural audio, manual QA, style linting, banned components, telemetry schemas, early snapshots, and conservative lifecycle assumptions.

- **Code review guardrails:** Review rejects client personality writes, user-facing trait numbers, welcome toasts, streaks, badges, recorded audio fallback, forbidden analytics, visitor drift, frozen reduced-motion, mechanical narration, and scene layouts that pan, scroll, zoom, crop birds, or place chrome inside the aviary.
