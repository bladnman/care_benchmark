## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Affective whys did slightly better by weight (68 / 98 = 69.4%) than functional whys (34 / 54 = 63.0%). The functional misses were often architectural failure-mode layers: F16 did not recover the retrofit/source-cutoff consequence, and F18 did not recover the silent lost-drift scenario.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys underperformed weight-2 whys (64.8% vs 72.7%). The planner preserved many complex mechanisms, but the frozen reconstruction often dropped layer 3 consequences such as stale-app leakage, divergent-client corruption, or disguised engagement loops.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** Feature-level fidelity (69.4%) exceeded system-level fidelity (57.1%). The plan was highly explicit at the implementation layer, while B's system summary compressed several named philosophies into broader categories like non-gamification, privacy, or accessibility.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes survived most often; secondary contributors survived when they were implementation-specific; downstream consequences were the most common leak. S1, S6, S7, F1, F2, F13, F16, and F18 all lost important consequence layers.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Audio, privacy telemetry, reduced motion, notebook, and age-gated adoption recovered well. Stable identity, export-as-relationship-copy, visitor actualness, and starter-birds-as-arrivals leaked because the plan stated the product rule more strongly than the affective rationale.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Several apparent recoveries were denied or capped because the reconstruction had rule evidence without why evidence: F11, F23, F29, F31, F34, and F39 are the clearest single-layer examples. The stricter v06 gate mattered.

What this suggests: the candidate plan is implementation-complete and unusually good at preserving concrete constraints, but its encoding is not equally lossless for affective and downstream-consequence rationales. The reconstruction could often say what to build; it less reliably recovered why a tempting alternative would break the product.
