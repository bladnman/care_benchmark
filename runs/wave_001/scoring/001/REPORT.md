## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better (34/54 weighted, 63.0%) than affective whys (49/96 weighted, 51.0%). The plan was strong on architecture and data boundaries; the reconstruction often kept mechanism while losing relational intent in F8, F11, F29, F31, F38, and F39.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered at 56/108 weighted (51.9%), below weight-2 whys at 27/42 (64.3%). High-weight feature whys often had three-layer rationales that the plan compressed into implementation rules, especially F30, F31, and F35.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System philosophy survived much better (75.0%) than feature-level intent (50.8%). B reconstructed the product posture well, but per-feature whys frequently collapsed to what to build.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary causes survived most often; secondary and downstream consequences leaked. Examples: F16 kept synthetic UUID but lost PII-sprawl and retrofit rationale; F18 kept additive deltas but lost the invisible LWW data-loss scenario.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync did well on server authority and telemetry (S7, S8, F17, F36) but weaker on user-facing account surfaces (F19 unreachable, F34/F35 none). Accessibility recovered strongly for reduced motion and TTFB (F25, F26) but partially for screen-reader narration nuance (F24, F40). Social sharing kept controls but lost why in comparison/show-off/log cases (F23, F38, F39).
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The strict evidence gate denied credit to plausible but absent rationales. `rule_without_why` was the main failure mode: the candidate often told a builder what to implement, but not why that rule protected the product relationship.

The failure shape suggests an excellent implementation planner whose compression preserves architecture and explicit refusals, while losing the less operational affective rationale behind many social, identity, and relationship-protection features.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more feature-level exception whys around relational privacy, social exposure, and identity continuity. These were where mechanism survived but intent leaked most clearly.
- Keep multi-layer whys, but consider requiring the phase-1 plan format to include a `why this rule exists` field for exception/refusal features. This would test whether models can preserve rationale without forcing verbose prose everywhere.
- Retain the system-level cross-cutting bar. It separated strong philosophy preservation (S7/S8/S9) from merely named principles (S3/S6) in a useful way.
- Add an explicit scoring diagnostic for `architecture strong, affective why weak`; this run shows a high-planning candidate can still under-encode the product's emotional contract.
- Keep the F29-F40 targeted headroom set. F30, F31, F35, F38, and F39 created meaningful separation that the original functional table might have missed.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The scorer received a fresh-context instruction and did not use memory, subagents, PRD, peer slots, or other waves. The reconstruction was treated as frozen.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run; no variance signal is available from this artifact alone.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive calls were made for the glossary, low-pass drift implementation, single-scene/no-panning, evening palette/call-quieting, and email-change flow. These did not affect feature-level reachability except indirectly through planning quality; the key denominator exclusion was F19.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The (b) judgment was easiest for S7, S8, and S9 and most subjective for S3/S4/S6. The appendix lists the exact PLAN inheritance locations used.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction was found. The reconstruction was conservative and often honest about non-recovery.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Apparent recoveries such as F8, F11, F29, F31, F35, F38, and F39 were denied because the reconstruction carried the rule but not the gold rationale.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** Eleven included whys were marked rule-without-why, the dominant source of feature-level fidelity loss.
- **Timing.** `TIMING.json` supplied phase1 and phase2a timings for run 001 only; no phase2b timing was available in the bounded ledger at write time.
