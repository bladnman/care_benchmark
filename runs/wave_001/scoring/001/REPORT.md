## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered at 92.6% by weight, while affective whys recovered at 87.8%. The main functional loss was F34, where export survived as data portability but not as a quiet copy of the user's relationship. The main affective losses were subtler: S2, S3, S4, F14, F27, and F30 compressed relational rationale into anti-engagement rules.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered at 90.7%, better than weight-2 whys at 86.4%. The high-weight material was usually repeated across architecture, tests, and risks, making it easier for B to recover.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** Feature-level fidelity (90.3%) slightly exceeded system-level fidelity (85.7%). The reconstruction was excellent at following the plan's section-by-section mechanisms, but less consistent at lifting every cross-cutting affective philosophy back into named principles.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary layers usually survived. Missing layers were mostly secondary affective explanation or downstream consequence: the processed-vs-seen layer in S2, the cheap-engagement temptation in F27, and the economy-collapse consequence in F30.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, privacy, sync, audio, accessibility, and visit boundaries scored strongly. The thin areas were opt-in rituals, exports, and anti-engagement refusals where the plan specified durable enforcement but the reconstruction often summarized the enforcement more than the relational reason.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** A v1-style scorer might have credited more rows because the rules were present. Variant v06 denied or reduced credit where the exact why was absent: F11 and F34 scored none; F14, F27, and F30 scored partial.

The failure shape suggests a strong implementation plan that over-preserves architecture and under-preserves a few delicate affective explanations.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the v06 evidence-bound operator. This run shows why it matters: planning was perfect on feature capture, but why preservation still had measurable losses.
- Add more targeted headroom around rule-without-why zones. Opt-in rituals, quiet account tools, and anti-engagement refusals are especially likely to survive as constraints while losing their relational rationale.
- For system-level whys, include more examples that force the reconstructor to promote plan-wide evidence back into a named principle. S3 and S4 were preserved cross-cuttingly in PLAN but not identified by B as system-level intent.
- Keep multi-layer scoring for high-value affective whys. The missing layers were diagnostically useful and revealed compression patterns that a single-layer rubric would hide.
- In future benchmark instances, include at least one product with less architecture-heavy privacy/sync content. This plan's engineering richness made functional why recovery unusually strong.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This scorer had fresh context for phase 2B and read only allowed phase-two files, the assigned plan, assigned metadata, the assigned timing slice, and the frozen reconstruction. No PRD, peer slots, or other waves were read.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance signal. The score should be compared across waves/runs before drawing model-level conclusions.
- **Borderline capture calls.** There were no meaningful borderline capture calls; all 120 features were explicitly addressed in the plan.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The strict three-feature bar mattered for S3 and S4: both were visibly preserved in PLAN, but B did not identify them strongly enough in the system-level section, producing partial recovery rather than full.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction was counted. The reconstruction reads plan-derived.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F11 and F34 were denied because the frozen reconstruction did not carry the gold rationale. F14, F16, F27, and F30 were reduced to partial for missing layers.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** F11, F14, F27, F30, and F34 are the main mechanism-only or rule-heavy survivals.
- **Operational compromise.** TIMING.json contained phase1 and phase2A data for run 001, but no phase2B timing entry at scoring time. The score JSON includes only the available bounded timing fields.
