## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered at 27.0 / 48.0 weighted points, while affective whys recovered at 23.0 / 82.0. The candidate carried architecture-heavy intent better than product-relationship intent.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** High-weight whys recovered 42.0 / 96.0 versus 8.0 / 34.0 for weight-2. The strongest recovery was still partial-heavy, with many high-weight layers missing their downstream consequences.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System fidelity (50.0%) exceeded feature fidelity (35.3%). Broad principles like server-authored continuity and accessibility-as-designed-surface survived better than narrow exceptions like no toast, opt-in settle, no friend-visited notification, account export, and deletion.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanism layers survived most often. Secondary calibration or temptation layers and downstream product-collapse consequences were frequently absent.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and performance produced the clearest recoveries. Social, notebook, visit-log, and announcement-restraint whys mostly survived as rules or not at all.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Many apparent recoveries were denied because the reconstruction named only the mechanism: F5, F6, F14, F21, F23, F27, F29, F31-F33, and F36-F39.

The failure shape suggests a planner that is strong at enumerating implementation surfaces but compresses affective rationale and exception logic aggressively.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted feature-level exception whys. F29-F40 were useful: several were captured as rules, but almost none carried the rationale.
- Preserve multi-layer judging for high-weight whys. The layer split exposed a consistent pattern: primary mechanisms survived while secondary and downstream layers dropped.
- Add more social/anti-engagement exceptions in future instances. This run's largest losses clustered around quiet non-notification, no comparison, and social privacy surfaces.
- Keep `rule_without_why` as a first-class diagnostic. It sharply separated broad planning coverage from intent carry-through without inflating fidelity.
- Consider a stricter prompt nudge for planners to preserve rationale, not only requirements. The benchmark is functioning, but this run shows how easy it is for a competent plan to omit why-carrying prose.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This scorer used the frozen reconstruction and did not modify it. The reconstruction itself shows plan-shaped headings and no gold IDs.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one run from one candidate setting; no variance signal is available inside this score.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on settle-as-interaction (#10), mood persistence (#29), no streaks (#50), no-per-bird ML telemetry (#85), and visitor actual-aviary (#97). These increased planning quality but did not create fidelity credit without reconstruction evidence.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S1, S6, S8, and S9 involved judgment on partial layer recovery; S7 was the clearest full system recovery.
- **Confabulation cases.** I did not find clear ungrounded reconstruction claims; the reconstruction mostly marked missing whys honestly.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Rule-only mentions were common and scored as none for why recovery when the rationale was absent.
