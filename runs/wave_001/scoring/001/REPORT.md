## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional recovery (34.0/54.0) was somewhat stronger than affective recovery (52.0/92.0). The plan carried many affective rules, but the reconstruction often dropped the relationship rationale: F23, F29, F37, and F39 are examples.

- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 64.0/104.0, better than weight-2 whys at 22.0/42.0. The high-weight rows often had enough surrounding structure to recover at least one layer.

- **System-level vs feature-level.** The plan preserved philosophy better than feature-specific rationales. System-level fidelity is 85.7%, while feature-level fidelity is 52.5%.

- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanisms survived most often. Secondary calibration gaps and downstream failure consequences leaked: F1 lost the shortcut analysis, F2 lost the screensaver/Tamagotchi band, and F27 lost the future-erosion argument.

- **Subdomain patterns.** Server authority, privacy, and performance were comparatively strong. Social and relationship-protective whys were weaker, especially where the plan stated a rule but not the affective reason.

- **Evidence-bound effects.** v06 denied many plausible v1-style recoveries because reconstruction evidence was mechanism-only. F40 is the cleanest case: the reconstruction itself says `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN`, so no recovery credit is possible.

The failure shape suggests that this candidate can plan a buildable product nearly end to end, but its plan-to-reconstruction encoding compresses away many of the non-obvious whys that protect the product from becoming adjacent-product-shaped.

---
## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted feature-level headroom rows like F29-F40. They produced useful discrimination even with high planning quality.

- Add more exception-style whys where an implementation rule is obvious but the rationale is not, especially in social and relationship-protective areas.

- Split system-level S2-style judgments into two diagnostics: inherited no-announcement rules versus the explicit affective contrast between being noticed and being announced at.

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. It cleanly distinguished `what to build` from `why this shape matters`, which was the main signal in this run.

- Consider asking reconstructors to label mechanism-only rows explicitly. This would reduce scorer subjectivity around `rule_without_why` without giving them the gold list.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The frozen reconstruction reads as plan-derived and contains no gold IDs. This scorer did not read PRD files, peer slots, or other waves.

- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance estimate.

- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive capture affected features 3, 7, 23, 47, 52, 53, and 94. Even changing all seven to misses would not alter the main diagnostic: feature-level why recovery is far lower than system-level recovery.

- **System-level cross-cutting.** S2 and S6 were the subjective calls. Both had real inherited plan decisions but incomplete recovered rationale.

- **Confabulation cases.** None were counted as positive recovery. The reconstruction was conservative and plan-shaped.

- **Evidence-bound denials.** F5, F6, F8, F22, F23, F29, F30, F32, F33, F37, F39, and F40 are prominent mechanism-only or explicit non-recovery cases.

- **Rule-without-why cases.** 27 included whys had some rule/mechanism present while one or more rationale layers were absent.
