## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** The run did not show a simple affective-only collapse: both buckets recovered 16.0 weighted points. Functional rows had a smaller denominator, so their rate was higher. Affective losses were still more visible in rows such as F23, F24, F31, F36, and F40, where the plan preserved a mechanism but not the relationship rationale.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 30.0 / 84.0, while weight-2 whys recovered only 2.0 / 22.0. The high-weight rows survived better because their mechanisms were often architectural: presence, drift, server tick, no LWW, accessibility surfaces.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 28.6%; feature-level fidelity was 30.8%. The similar numbers mask different failures: system rows lost downstream consequences, while feature rows were often unreachable because the compact plan omitted greetings, layout, notebook, social, and account-management details.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Layer 1 survived most often. Layer 2 occasionally survived for server-side simulation, monotonic drift, and accessibility; layer 3 was almost always missing.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility had the best capture. Aviary layout, social details, return greetings, field notebook details, and account lifecycle features were mostly absent.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** v06 denied credit for many plausible-looking rows because the exact why was absent: cap at 7, synthetic UUID, first bird under 500ms, aggregate telemetry, stable bird identity, and narration cadence.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. This run shows a planner can name many mechanisms while losing the rationale that prevents ordinary product substitutions.
- Add targeted headroom around compressed exception rules: adoption/no-catalog, field notebook read-only, visit transparency, visitor actualness, and accessibility cadence.
- Preserve multi-layer scoring for high-risk whys. The largest leak was downstream consequence, especially why a tempting alternate implementation would become a different product.
- Consider making non-goal rationales first-class in future phase-1 instructions. The candidate listed non-goals, but often not why they matter.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The reconstruction reads plan-derived and plan-structured; it does not show gold-ID leakage or gold-list ordering.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on aliveness, mood persistence, read-only visitor implications, reduced-motion charm, captions, telemetry, and broad no-discovery/no-score surfaces. These calls raised planning quality but mostly added low- or zero-recovery denominator.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S1, S6, S7, and S9 cleared the >=3 inheritance bar; S2, S3, S4, S5, and S8 did not.
- **Confabulation cases.** I did not award credit for ungrounded claims. Some reconstruction wording was inferential, but no score depends on a rationale absent from PLAN.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Mechanism-only rows were common and are captured by the 17 `rule_without_why` cases.
