## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better: 36.0/54.0 weighted points (66.7%) versus affective whys at 54.0/94.0 (57.4%). The reconstruction handles server authority, privacy boundaries, telemetry, and performance rationales more faithfully than relationship-shape rationales.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 70.0/108.0 (64.8%), better than weight-2 whys at 20.0/40.0 (50.0%). High-weight architecture/calibration whys were easier to infer from the detailed plan.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (71.4%) was higher than feature-level fidelity (58.3%). The plan preserved philosophy, but the reconstruction compressed many feature-specific exceptions into generic implementation rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary causes usually survived; secondary affective contributors and downstream relationship consequences dropped most often. Examples: S2 loses processed-vs-seen, F16 loses PII-leak/retrofit layers, F40 loses screen-reader queue overload.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility performed best where architecture was concrete. Social and relationship-preserving surfaces leaked most: F22, F23, F39, and F29 are rule-only or not recovered.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** v06 denied credit where the reconstruction said what to build but not why: F5, F6, F8, F9, F22, F23, F29, F32, F34, F35, and F39 are the clearest rule-without-why cases.

The failure shape suggests the plan is implementation-rich, but not all feature-level rationale was encoded in a way a blind reconstructor could recover.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator; it prevented over-credit for rule-only recoveries.
- Add more feature-level exception whys in affective/social areas. This run shows that rules like no leaderboards, named invites, and no show-off mode can be implemented without preserving the relationship rationale.
- Keep multi-layer judging for calibration and architecture. It exposed exactly which layer was missing rather than collapsing F1/F13/F18/F40 into ambiguous partial prose.
- Consider adding a report-only rule-only evidence field separate from positive rationale evidence, while keeping loose score JSON qualitative-free.
- Clarify system-level scoring when the plan preserves a principle cross-cuttingly but the reconstruction names it only through per-feature mechanics, as with S4 and S5.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The frozen reconstruction was not modified. I did not read `prd/`, peer slots, or other waves.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is a single run; no variance signal is available inside slot 001.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive calls were applied to the glossary/domain terms (#7), no chat/comments/avatars (#93), visitor actual snapshot (#97), and palette scope (#69). These do not affect the feature-why denominator except where the gold anchor itself was captured.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 and S5 are the subjective cases: the PLAN visibly inherits them, but B did not reconstruct the full principle cleanly.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction cases were found. Some rationale was generic or thin, but still plan-derived.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several v1-style plausible recoveries were denied because they carried only the mechanism or only one layer of a multi-layer why.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** The primary diagnostic is rule-only survival: 13 included whys were marked `rule_without_why`.
