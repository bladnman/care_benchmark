## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better (24.0/42.0) than affective whys (31.0/78.0). The plan and reconstruction preserved explicit architecture such as server ticks, event logs, and drift calibration, while affective rationales around announcement refusal, specificity, privacy, and relationship boundaries often became mechanism-only rows.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 48.0/92.0; weight-2 whys recovered 7.0/28.0. The higher-weight whys were often attached to explicit implementation risks, while many weight-2 exceptions were short rules without rationale.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 53.6%, above feature-level fidelity at 43.5%. The reconstruction extracted broad architecture and accessibility principles better than targeted feature exceptions.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanism layers were most likely to survive. Secondary calibration stories and downstream relationship consequences were commonly lost, especially in F1, F4, F12, F13, F15, F16, F18, F24, F25, F31, and F40.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation and sync were strongest: F2 and F17 were full, with partial credit on F1, F7, F13, F18. Social and privacy were weak: F22, F38 were unreachable; F23, F37, F39 were rule-only; S8 was absent.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Fourteen included whys were marked `rule_without_why`, mostly where the plan listed the operational rule but not the relationship/affective reason. No ungrounded reconstruction cases were counted.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** I used only the allowlisted phase-two files, the assigned PLAN and metadata, assigned timing entry, assigned frozen reconstruction, and assigned scoring slot. I did not read `prd/`, peer slots, or other waves.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one sampled run; there is no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on several thin but plausible captures: voice/tone guide, starter birds, mood state, bird naming, several layout features, caption toggle, and narration cadence. These affect planning quality but not fidelity unless the why is reconstructed.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S3-S5 required judgment because the plan retained naturalist/restraint rules while dropping parts of the named rationale. The appendix above states the evidence trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** None were counted as ungrounded reconstruction. The reconstruction generally stayed close to PLAN text and often used `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN` rather than inventing missing rationales.
- **Timing.** `TIMING.json` contained phase 1 and phase 2A timings for run 001 only. I included those bounded fields and did not fabricate phase 2B timing.
