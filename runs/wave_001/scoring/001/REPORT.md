## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better by weight because architecture-heavy material like S7, S8, F18, F26, and F36 was explicit in the plan. Affective whys were more often reduced to rules: no streaks, no leaderboards, no catalog-like starter choice, and no show-off mode survived as prohibitions without the relationship-shaping rationale.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys often recovered their primary mechanism but dropped secondary temptation/failure-mode layers. Examples: F1 kept the conjunction but lost silent drift corruption; F27 kept the gamification ban but lost the “just one streak counter” slippery-slope rationale; F30 kept age-based growth but lost economy/reward framing.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The plan encoded broad philosophy well enough for strong system-level recovery, especially S1, S7, S8, and S9. Feature-level fidelity dropped because B frequently wrote `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN` or named only implementation rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary layers survived most often. Secondary affective justifications and downstream “wrong product” consequences were the most common losses.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Sync, telemetry, accessibility, and performance were strongest. Social and account-polish whys were weakest: F22, F23, F34, F37, F38, and F39 mostly retained mechanisms without intent.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter v06 gate denied credit where a v1-style read might have been generous: F6, F11, F14, F22, F23, F29-F34, and F37-F39 all preserve rules but not recoverable whys.

What this suggests: the candidate plan is operationally broad and disciplined, but its encoding is strongest when the plan states an architectural reason directly. It is much weaker when the original rationale is affective, relational, or phrased as a future temptation to avoid.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The prompt declares fresh context, and the reconstruction had no gold IDs or rubric vocabulary. I treated the frozen reconstruction as plan-derived.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one candidate plan and one reconstruction; no variance signal is available from this slot.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on features 4, 7, 25, 36, 37, 51, 57, and 93. These affect planning quality more than fidelity because most related whys still lacked rationale recovery.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 and S5 were the most subjective: the plan contained many local rules, but B did not recover the full restraint or system-exception rationale.
- **Confabulation.** I did not count any major ungrounded reconstruction as recovered. Apparent inferences such as F33 were treated as rule-only rather than credited.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Fourteen included whys were marked rule-without-why; these are the main reason feature-level fidelity trails planning quality.
- **Operational timing.** `TIMING.json` supplied phase 1 and phase 2A data for run 001 only; phase 2B timing was not yet available inside the scoring context.
