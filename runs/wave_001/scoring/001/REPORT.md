## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 18.0 / 44.0 (40.9%); affective whys recovered 29.0 / 80.0 (36.3%). Functional architecture around server state, sync, and privacy survived better than subtle affective exceptions like S2, F9, F10, F14, F23, F30, and F39.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 36.0 / 96.0 (37.5%), while weight-2 whys recovered 11.0 / 28.0 (39.3%). Multi-layer entries often retained one implementation layer but lost downstream failure modes.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity beat feature-level fidelity (46.4% vs 35.4%). The candidate preserved broad product posture more reliably than feature-specific rationale.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanisms survived most often. Secondary calibration survived when named in PLAN (F2's 1 week / 3 weeks), but downstream consequences were repeatedly dropped: F2 screensaver endpoint, F4 audio-spine cascade, F17 client-side collapse, and F31 relationship-continuity loss.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility scored comparatively well; interactions/social carried many rule-only or unreachable entries. The plan named Return-greeting, Offer, and Settle, but without the detailed affordance rules that make those interaction whys recoverable.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** V06 denied likely v1-style credit for mechanism-only rows. Examples: F6 cap=7, F13 presence accounting, F14 no streaks, F21 read-only visit, F23 no public discovery, F26 <500ms, F30 age-based offers, and F37 per-email invitations.

The failure shape suggests the candidate can compress a PRD into plausible architecture, but it loses the product's rationale layer whenever that rationale is not already a familiar engineering constraint.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. This run demonstrates that mechanism capture and rationale recovery are meaningfully different signals.
- Preserve targeted exception whys. The misses around no welcome toast, no visit notification, no leaderboards, no editable notebook, and no reward unlocks are exactly the places a plausible product plan tends to drift toward generic engagement patterns.
- Add a non-scoring diagnostic for rule-only capture in consolidators. It would make it easier to compare compact plans without putting qualitative labels in the strict loose JSON.
- Continue using multi-layer whys for central product claims. The downstream-consequence layer exposed the largest compression loss.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The scorer treated the reconstruction as frozen, did not modify it, and stayed inside the supplied allowlists. No `prd/`, peer slots, or other waves were read.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run only; no variance signal is available.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive for mechanism-level references such as return-greeting, mood persistence, field notebook, presence accounting, visitor actual-aviary snapshot, and reduced-motion charm. These calls increased planning quality but did not create why recovery without evidence.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S2, S3, and S6 came down to the strict three-feature bar: adjacent rules existed, but the named principle did not visibly shape enough plan decisions.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear contamination-style confabulation was found. Thin inference was handled by denying full recovery unless reconstruction evidence and PLAN grounding both existed.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** 14 included whys were marked rule-without-why; these remained in the denominator and mostly scored none.
