## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys did better (59.3%) than affective whys (47.9%). Server/canonical-state and privacy boundary reasoning survived; relationship-framing whys around announcements, comparison, visitor surfaces, and export leaked.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 53.8%, while weight-2 whys recovered 47.7%. Higher-weight whys were not automatically safe: many multi-layer whys lost secondary or downstream layers.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level intent (67.9%) outperformed feature-level intent (48.3%). The plan encoded broad philosophy better than per-feature exception rationale.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary mechanism layers often survived; secondary contributor and downstream-consequence layers were most often missing. This is visible in F1, F2, F3, F12, F13, F14, F20, F24, F25, F30, F35, and F40.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and privacy were comparatively strong. Social optional and small interaction surfaces were weakest because the reconstruction kept the rule but dropped the reason.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** V06 denied credit where the plan had implementation detail but B did not recover the why, especially F5, F6, F8, F19, F21, F22, F23, F29, F34, and F39.

The failure shape suggests that this candidate plan is strong as a builder-facing implementation plan, but not consistently strong as an intent-carrying artifact. It carries architecture better than affective product philosophy at the feature-exception level.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more headroom around interaction micro-rationales. Greeting variation, settle, offer cooldown/reactions, and visit notifications are easy to implement mechanically while losing why.
- Keep targeted feature-level exceptions. F29-F40 were useful: account export, deletion, sharing, visit logs, and narration cadence exposed compression not visible in the older surface.
- Keep v06 evidence-bound scoring. It separated high capture from weaker rationale carry-through and prevented PLAN-only rationale from inflating recovery.
- Consider clearer guidance for system-level multi-layer scoring. S1/S2/S9 were recognizable as principles but hard to distinguish between principle recovery and all-layer recovery.
- In future product instances, include more whys where a feature is an exclusion. Exclusions produce many rule-without-why cases and are high-signal for carry-through.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This scoring pass treated the frozen reconstruction as read-only and did not modify it. No subagents were spawned.
- **Single-run limitation.** This is one candidate output and one reconstruction; there is no variance estimate.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive calls included starter auto-selection, settle opt-in, single-scene no-panning, bird-chosen perch, nightjar night state, calm palette, social no-chat/avatar, and visitor actual-aviary. These raised planning quality but did not create why recovery without reconstruction evidence.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The cross-cutting bar was most subjective for S1, S2, S4, and S9; the appendix lists the inheritance trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction claims were found. The reconstruction reads plan-derived.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several v1-style semantic matches were denied or reduced because B preserved only the mechanism.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** There were 15 included rule-without-why cases; this is the central diagnostic pattern.
