## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better: **42 / 54** weighted points (77.8%) versus affective **64 / 98** (65.3%). - **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered **78 / 108** (72.2%), above weight-2 whys at **28 / 44** (63.6%). The stronger weighted whys were often tied to implementation invariants, but many still lost one layer.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The candidate preserved philosophy better than specific feature rationales: S-level **26 / 28** weighted points versus F-level **80 / 124**. The reconstruction identified the large architectural themes, while detail rows frequently compressed away the reason.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Missing layers were usually secondary affective contributors: the well-meaning-toast risk in F10, intent rotation in F14, same-right-to-feel in F24, accessibility-cost consequence in F25, relationship-deepens-over-time in F30, and visual-rhythm layer in F40.
