## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better (44/54) than affective whys (72/98). The largest affective leaks were social/lifecycle rationale rows: F22, F23, F33, F34, F35, and F37.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 82/108; weight-2 whys recovered 34/44. High-weight rows usually preserved the primary mechanism but often lost one secondary consequence.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The planner preserved philosophy better than individual feature rationales: 26/28 system weight vs 90/124 feature weight. System S2 was the only non-full system row.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** The most common loss was layer 2 or layer 3: F2 lost the failure-mode band, F10 lost the toast temptation and variant spread, F20 lost the private-relationship-as-data-product layer, and F40 lost the user-event priority layer.
- **Subdomain pattern.** Core simulation, audio, rendering, privacy infrastructure, and accessibility were strong. Social sharing and account lifecycle surfaces were weaker, especially where the rule was obvious but the product-relationship rationale was subtle.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Six apparent feature presences were denied why credit as rule-only. No recovery was denied for ungrounded reconstruction; the reconstruction stayed grounded in the PLAN.

What this suggests: this plan is a near-complete implementation spec, and the reconstruction captured its major architecture. The benchmark still found useful signal in whether the deeper why survived for targeted policy/social/lifecycle features.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This phase-2B pass used only the allowed phase-two files, the assigned plan, candidate metadata, slot timing, and frozen reconstruction. I did not read `prd/`, peer slots, or other waves.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run, with no variance estimate.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Feature 7 was the only borderline capture call: the plan did not include a standalone glossary, but it defined bird/call/mood/settle concepts throughout the implementation plan. Planning quality was not meaningfully affected.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The (b) judgment used the strict three-feature bar; all S1-S9 principles were preserved cross-cuttingly in PLAN. S2 remained partial because one affective layer was missing from the reconstruction.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no ungrounded reconstruction cases.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** The main denials were rule-without-why rows where the PLAN/reconstruction preserved mechanics but not the gold rationale.
- **Operational compromise.** `TIMING.json` supplied phase 1 and phase 2A timing only for run 001; unavailable phase 2B timing fields were omitted rather than fabricated.
