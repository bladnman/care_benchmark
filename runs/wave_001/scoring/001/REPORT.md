## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Recovery was similar by type: functional 42/54, affective 78/98. The sharper distinction was mechanism versus rationale. F8, F11, F19, F29, and F39 preserved rules but not the relationship-level reason.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered slightly better (86/108) than weight-2 whys (34/44), mostly because the plan's structural architecture made high-weight system and engine whys easy to reconstruct.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level recovery was strong (26/28). Feature-level recovery was lower (94/124) because targeted headroom whys exposed compression at the edges.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes survived most often. Secondary affective contributors and downstream product-consequence layers were the most common losses, especially F10, F14, F30, and F31.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Engine, sync, accessibility, and performance were very strong. Social and account-tail exceptions had the most rule-without-why loss.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The v06 evidence gate denied full credit for plausible mechanism matches where the reconstruction did not state the gold why. This mattered most for F8, F11, F19, F29, F35, and F39.

The failure shape suggests an excellent implementation plan that carries architectural intent better than delicate affective exceptions.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep F29-F40-style headroom whys. They separated strong planners from plans/reconstructions that only preserve the obvious mechanics.
- Add more exception whys where the tempting implementation rule is easy but the reason is not: numeric hiding, opt-in rituals, actual visitor rendering, and matter-of-fact system voice.
- Preserve evidence-bound scoring. Without it, mechanism-only rows such as F11 and F19 would be easy to over-credit.
- Consider adding a small required "why capsule" convention to phase-1 planning prompts in future benchmark variants, then measure whether it increases reconstruction fidelity without bloating plans.
