## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered **26.0 / 48** weighted points (54.2%), while affective whys recovered **39.0 / 82** (47.6%). Functional architecture survived when it was explicit in the plan, especially S7, S8, F2, and F36. Affective exceptions often compressed into rules: F28 excluded Tamagotchi mechanics, but the absence-punishment and obligation rationale did not survive.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered **52.0 / 92** (56.5%), much better than weight-2 whys at **13.0 / 38** (34.2%). The multi-layer structure helped catch partial recovery rather than flattening everything to none.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was **67.9%**, feature-level fidelity was **45.1%**. The reconstructor extracted broad principles such as server-owned state, privacy boundaries, and accessibility as affective core, but feature exceptions such as F16, F21, F23, F29, F33, and F37 lost their specific rationale.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary rule layers survived most often. Secondary failure modes and downstream consequences were commonly dropped: F30 recovered age-not-attention but missed unlock-economy erosion; F40 recovered slow cadence and priority bump but missed screen-reader queue overload.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation/sync and accessibility were strongest. Arrival greeting was a planning miss: F9, F10, and related greeting variants were unreachable. Account lifecycle was also thin: export and deletion whys were unreachable.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter evidence gate denied credit where the PLAN had a mechanism but RECONSTRUCTION did not carry the why. There were **16** included `rule_without_why` cases and no clear ungrounded-reconstruction cases.

What this suggests: the candidate can produce a broad implementation map and preserve several architectural invariants, but it compresses affective product intent aggressively unless the plan states the rationale in plain operational terms.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted headroom additions like F29-F40. They exposed a real gap between implementing a rule and preserving its rationale.
- Add or retain why anchors around exclusions that look like ordinary implementation constraints. F16, F21, F23, F28, F33, and F37 were all vulnerable to rule-only survival.
- Keep multi-layer scoring for high-weight whys. It produced useful partial-credit signal for F2, F4, F24, F25, F30, and F40 instead of reducing all nuanced cases to binary outcomes.
- Consider making arrival/return-greeting a capture-critical cluster in future instances. This run missed the greeting surface entirely, removing a major affective area from feature-level reachability.
- Preserve the evidence-bound operator. It prevented plan-only or rule-only rows from inflating fidelity and made the system/feature diagnostic split meaningful.
