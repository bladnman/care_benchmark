- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was high (85.7%) because the plan encoded principles as repeated invariants and tooling. Feature-level fidelity was much lower (53.2%): the reconstruction frequently described what to build without the narrower gold why.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** L1 layers survived most often. L2 survived when it was expressed as architecture or a concrete failure mode (presence inflation, chorus artifacts, sync conflicts). L3 downstream consequence was most often dropped, especially where the consequence was affective rather than operational.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility were strongest. Bird engine was mixed: drift, identity, and audio had partial recovery, while vector numeric exposure and starter-bird framing lost rationale. Social was weakest: visits, leaderboards, per-invite sharing, and actual-aviary sharing were mostly rule-only.
