## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered at 52.1%, ahead of affective whys at 36.6%. The plan encoded architecture and telemetry boundaries better than affective boundaries like no-toast, notebook read-only, and private social sharing.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered at 44.0%, better than weight-2 whys at 36.7%. Load-bearing architecture such as S7, F17, and F18 survived better than small exception whys.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (60.7%) beat feature-level fidelity (37.3%). The plan preserved broad philosophy but often dropped the feature-specific rationale needed to reconstruct exact whys.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Layer 1 mechanisms survived most often. Layer 2 calibration examples and Layer 3 downstream product-consequence arguments were usually missing.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accessibility and server/sync were strongest. Interactions, social defaults, account lifecycle, and layout detail leaked most.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The main denied class was rule_without_why: 16 included whys preserved a mechanism without enough rationale for recovery.

What the failure shape suggests: this planner was good at implementable architecture, strong non-goals, and accessibility scope, but it converted many CARE-relevant affective whys into terse product rules.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the v06 evidence-bound ledger. It made the distinction between feature capture and why recovery explicit, especially for F6, F8, F14, F23, F27, F29, F31, F37, and F38.
- Add more exception whys around tiny UI/social surfaces. This run missed no-toast, no visit notification, read-only notebook, no show-off mode, and sync-conflict tone despite broad non-goals.
- Clarify partial credit for single-layer system whys when a mechanism survives but the rationale does not. S5 and S8 required judgment calls.
- Retain multi-layer high-weight whys. They exposed the dominant compression pattern: primary rule survives, calibration and consequence disappear.
- Consider adding plan-writing prompts that ask for rationale on every explicit exclusion. The plan said what not to build, but often not why the exclusion was product-defining.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The frozen reconstruction reads plan-derived: it mirrors PLAN sections and contains no gold IDs. The validity audit records PASS.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Two calls were inclusive: feature 25 (starter birds treated as auto-selected enough) and feature 93 (no social network inferred as no chat/comments/avatars). These move planning quality by at most 1.7 percentage points.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The three-feature bar was subjective for S4, S5, and S8; the cross-cutting appendix records the judgment trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction was counted. Some phrases were synthesized but grounded in PLAN.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Many v1-style semantic recoveries were denied because they preserved operational rules without gold rationale.
- **Operational compromise.** TIMING.json supplied phase 1 and phase 2A timing for run 001 only; phase 2B timing was not fabricated.
