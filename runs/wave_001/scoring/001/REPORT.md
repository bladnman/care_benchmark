## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Affective whys recovered better: 83.0/98.0 versus 40.0/54.0 functional. The main functional leaks were presence precision and account/sync failure modes (F1, F13, F16, F18, F35).
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 84.0/108.0, below weight-2 at 39.0/44.0. Multi-layer targets exposed compression: the reconstruction often kept L1 and lost a downstream failure-mode layer.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** Both were high, but system-level was slightly stronger at 23.0/28.0 than feature-level at 100.0/124.0. The PLAN encoded philosophy very well; the reconstruction shortened some feature exceptions.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** The most frequently dropped layer was downstream consequence, especially where the gold included what would happen if the rule slipped.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Audio, accessibility, drift, and core continuity were strong. Presence/account/privacy mechanics were implemented strongly but sometimes lost the deeper why.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** F19 and F22 looked plausible at the rule level, but neither reconstruction nor PLAN carried the full rationale; v06 correctly held them at none.

What this suggests: the candidate PLAN is extremely buildable and intent-rich, but a fresh reconstructor compresses dense multi-layer rationales into implementation constraints unless the plan repeats the why in plain explanatory prose.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted headroom whys. They prevented this near-complete plan from saturating the benchmark.
- Add more account/privacy/social exception whys; those areas showed rule survival with thinner rationale transfer.
- Preserve multi-layer judging, especially for downstream-consequence layers. That is where high-capability plans and reconstructions diverged most.
- Clarify whether system-level recovery may use per-feature reconstruction evidence, or only the `## System-level intent` section. S2 and S6 were the most sensitive to that interpretation.
- Reconsider documentation-like capture rows such as the glossary; it behaved differently from product and engineering features.
