## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better because the plan encoded architecture and measurable constraints: S7, S8, F2, F18, and F36 all had concrete event-log, server-authority, or telemetry-boundary hooks. Affective whys often survived as prohibitions without purpose: F14, F23, F27, F29, F30, F33, F37, and F39 are mostly rule-only.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** High-weight whys did not uniformly survive. F2 and S7 were strong, but F14, F27, F30, and F31 show that multi-layer affective exceptions are where compression did the most damage.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System philosophy outperformed feature-level rationale: 67.9% vs 38.5%. The planner carried broad architecture and accessibility principles, but lost many local reasons for social and interaction exclusions.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Primary mechanism layers survived most often. Secondary calibration details and downstream consequence layers were regularly dropped, especially where the gold why warned against a product becoming a different product.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation/sync and accessibility were strongest. Social, account lifecycle, voice exception, visit-notification/log surfaces, and field-notebook rationale were weaker.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter operator denied plausible v1-style credit where only a rule survived. Examples: F6 cap-7, F11 settle, F14 streaks, F26 first-bird performance, F30 age-based birds, and F33 read-only notebook.

The failure shape suggests the candidate is good at turning PRD features into an implementation map, but weaker at preserving the product's causal/affective rationale unless that rationale is expressed as architecture, timing, or a risk.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The reconstruction appears fresh-context and plan-derived: no gold IDs or rubric vocabulary were found, and the heading structure follows the plan rather than the gold list.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one candidate run; it gives no variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on several compressed features: mood daily-ish, settle lighting, background-tab pause, day/night, weather, ambient drift, visits default-off, visitor restrictions, no chat/comments, and visitor actual-aviary. Tightening those would lower planning quality modestly but not change the fidelity shape.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The most subjective calls were S3 and S5. I gave S3 full credit because naturalist specificity appears across multiple surfaces, while S5 only partial because the matter-of-fact system exception is absent.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction was scored as recovery. The reconstruction generally stayed close to the plan and often said `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN` where appropriate.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Many entries were denied because exact why evidence was missing despite captured rules: F6, F11, F14, F21, F23, F26, F27, F29-F33, F37, and F39.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** The dominant pattern is mechanism survival without rationale, especially in social, gamification, and small affective exceptions.
