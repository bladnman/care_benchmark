## System-level intent

1. **Thin-client, thick-server authority for canonical state.** The plan names a "thin-client, thick-server model" and repeatedly places truth on the server: the server maintains the "canonical state database," the tick engine processes the "canonical aviary status," clients "maintain no local simulation clock," and devices "do not write state values."

2. **Slow, organic change rather than action-driven progression.** Growth and drift are deliberately paced: bird adoption is "unlocked purely by aviary age," rollout says this is "to prevent user fatigue and ensure organic growth," and personality drift uses an alpha that is a "decay constraint ensuring changes take weeks."

3. **Calm, non-gamified, non-punitive product shape.** The non-goals reject "XP, streaks, levels, visit counters, milestone calendars," and "visual validation of 'daily visits.'" They also reject custodial pressure: birds "cannot die, get hungry, show distress, or decay in mood due to user absence."

4. **Naturalist, sparse, non-canned presentation.** The plan's voice avoids canned feedback: the return greeting is "Non-canned," the Field Notebook contains "Naturalist, sparse observations," procedural calls avoid canned audio loops, and screen-reader output uses prose such as "a warbler is resting on the front perch."

5. **Presence matters, but only when it is real and integrity-protected.** The plan requires a "visible tab, focus, and pointer/keyboard activity window," verifies continuous `presence_ping` intervals, pauses event recording during dropout, avoids "long-term offline cache to prevent invalid drift spoofing," and drops unsaved events "to maintain simulation integrity."

6. **Privacy-preserving single-user design with deliberately limited sharing.** The plan specifies encrypted PII, `email_hash` lookup, `202 Accepted` to protect against email enumeration, "secure unique links," "read-only" visits, and no "public discovery feeds, shared profiles, comments, avatars, or co-presence."

7. **Accessibility as a parallel surface, not an afterthought.** The plan includes reduced motion, prose screen-reader narration, dynamic procedural call captions, keyboard navigation, and a WebAudio fallback that "automatically enables visual Call Captions."

8. **Procedural, local sensory rendering under performance budgets.** Visual rendering, animation, and WebAudio are client responsibilities, while procedural audio avoids canned loops. The plan sets bundle, render-time, FPS, and memory budgets, including "< 500ms time-to-first-bird render" and a JS bundle under 2MB gzipped.

9. **Conflict avoidance by design.** The sync model "avoids conflict resolution workflows" by keeping clients stateless, making clients refresh visual parameters "ONLY by downloading state updates from the server," and ensuring multiple devices receive the "exact state matching the last completed tick."

10. **Operational observability bounded by privacy.** Metrics include latency, transaction duration, WebAudio errors, FPS drops, and auth failure percentages, but "Forbidden Metrics" are "Never Collected," including user-specific bird choices, click frequencies, and raw email inputs.

## Per-feature whys

### 1. Scope

#### Aviary Scene & Bird Lifecycle

- **One horizontal, single-screen responsive aviary canvas per user account:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN

#### Interactions

- **Return-Greeting:** The plan frames this as a "Non-canned, staggered greeting" on reconnect, personalized by "length of user absence and individual bird boldness."
- **Listen-in:** The plan calls this a "Focus mode" whose purpose is to lower other birds to "low ambient volume" while raising "the focused bird's procedural sound."
- **Offers:** The per-bird cooldown exists "to protect drift metrics."
- **Settle:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN
- **Field Notebook:** The plan's rationale is a sparse naturalist record: "Naturalist, sparse observations (~1 every few days) generated by the server," with tick criteria such as a "first greeting order shift."

#### Core Systems

- **Single-user account magic-link authentication:** The plan ties this to session and privacy behavior: 15-minute expiry, single-use tokens, encrypted email storage, and `202 Accepted` "to protect user privacy from email enumeration."
- **Server-side simulation tick:** The tick processes the "canonical aviary status" at a slow cadence and consumes event streams to update "personality drift and mood states."
- **Client presence tracking:** Presence requires visible tab, focus, and activity because the tick verifies a valid "user session segment" through continuous `presence_ping` signals before applying drift.
- **Read-only visit system:** The why is controlled sharing: it shares "the host aviary state" through "secure unique links," while visitors "cannot interact or see other visitors."

#### Accessibility & Performance

- **Reduced-motion mode:** The plan says it replaces "high-frequency animations with slow cross-fades of posture."
- **Prose-based screen-reader narration:** The plan uses prose narration for screen-reader access and later mitigates bloat with a "strict 45-second debounce" so updates do not "flood screen reader queues."
- **Dynamic procedural call captioning:** The plan places captions "visual-adjacent to birds" and makes them the automatic fallback when WebAudio initialization fails.
- **Initial JavaScript and time-to-first-bird targets:** The plan sets these so the app meets payload and first-render limits: initial JS under 2MB gzipped and "< 500ms time-to-first-bird render."

#### Out-of-Scope v1 Non-Goals

- **No native applications:** The plan's rationale is browser scope: "standard mobile/desktop browsers only."
- **No gamification:** The rationale is to avoid "XP, streaks, levels, visit counters, milestone calendars," and "visual validation of 'daily visits.'"
- **No custodial needs:** The plan rejects absence punishment: birds "cannot die, get hungry, show distress, or decay in mood due to user absence."
- **No social networks:** The plan limits social behavior by excluding "public discovery feeds, shared profiles, comments, avatars, or co-presence."

### 2. Architecture & Service Shape

#### Client/Server Split

- **Thin-client, thick-server model:** The plan uses this split to keep auth, session management, PII encryption, simulation, drift, mood state, canonical data, and notebook prose on the server.
- **Server auth, session management, and PII encryption:** The why is privacy and account control, carried by "PII encryption" and session management responsibilities.
- **Server simulation loop:** The server evaluates the "cron-style simulation loop (Tick)" so state changes happen against the canonical database.
- **Server consumption of client event streams:** The plan says event streams are consumed "to compute and update personality drift and mood states."
- **Client rendering and animation:** The client owns "rendering visual assets," viewport-responsive dimensions, sprites, and interpolation, so the browser handles presentation while the server holds truth.
- **Client WebAudio synthesis:** The client synthesizes procedural calls locally "based on motif structures sent by the server," supporting procedural audio without server-streamed audio assets.
- **Client presence calculation:** The client calculates presence requirements through "Tab Visibility API, Document Focus, and activity event listener."
- **Client interpolation between ticks:** The purpose is "smooth animation" between discrete server ticks.

### 3. Data Model

#### Database Schema

- **Accounts table with strict synthetic UUID isolation:** The plan names "Strict Synthetic UUID Isolation" and stores `encrypted_email` plus `email_hash`, supporting encrypted PII with a lookup index rather than raw email lookup.
- **Sessions table:** The rationale is only the server responsibility of "session management"; beyond token hash, last seen, and revocation fields, no more specific why is recoverable.
- **Aviary state table:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN
- **Birds table:** The table "Holds Canonical Personality Vectors and Current Moods," matching the plan's server-side drift and mood simulation.
- **Append-only interaction event log:** The plan labels it "Source of truth for ticks."
- **Field Notebook table:** The table stores generated notebook prose as `prose_content`, matching "Field Notebook prose observations."
- **Social invites table:** The table supports "secure unique links" with hashed visitor email, expiration, and revocation for read-only visits.

### 5. Simulation Engine Design

#### Server-Side Tick Loop

- **Once-per-minute backend worker:** The cadence supports a slow simulation loop that processes the canonical aviary status.
- **Retrieve Unprocessed Events:** This pulls records from `interaction_events`, which the plan calls the "Source of truth for ticks."
- **Verify Presence Window:** The plan requires continuous `presence_ping` signals with interval validation no more than 35 seconds to count a "valid user session segment."
- **Apply Personality Drift:** Drift uses presence and interactions to update vector traits while the formula's `alpha = 10^-5` is a "decay constraint ensuring changes take weeks."
- **Asymmetrical drift term `(1 - x)`:** The plan says this "bounds x below 1.0 and prevents negative updates (asymmetry requirement)."
- **Evaluate Mood Shifts:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN
- **Write Snapshots & Notebook logs:** The tick commits results and writes "sparse log observations when criteria match," such as "first greeting order shift."

#### Call-Grammar Motif Runtime

- **Client-side call configuration from tick seed:** The plan connects each bird's call configuration to a random seed generated during the tick, preserving procedural sound generation.
- **Motif representation:** NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN
- **Drift and mood influence on calls:** The plan makes personality and mood audible: high `vocal_frequency` shrinks silence intervals, `drowsy` lowers pitch and increases pauses, and `alert` adds sharp volume spikes.

### 6. Sync Model

#### Event Ordering & Snapshot Updates

- **Stateless client pattern:** The plan explicitly says Pocket Aviary "avoids conflict resolution workflows" this way.
- **No local simulation clock:** The plan prevents divergent clients by saying clients maintain "no local simulation clock."
- **Immediate event dispatch to `/api/aviary/events`:** The server commits the raw event immediately, keeping interaction facts server-side.
- **State refresh only from server:** The plan says the client refreshes visual parameters "ONLY by downloading state updates from the server."
- **Concurrent clients:** Multiple devices do not conflict because they receive "the exact state matching the last completed tick" and "do not write state values."

#### Handling Network Dropout

- **Pausing event recording and volatile buffering:** The plan avoids a "long-term offline cache to prevent invalid drift spoofing."
- **Matter-of-fact connection alert:** The plan gives the alert text so the user knows "We couldn't sync your actions" and to reload when signal returns.
- **Dropping unsaved events:** The plan says unsaved events are dropped "to maintain simulation integrity."

### 12. Risks and Mitigations

#### Calibration of Personality Drift

- **Offline simulations over 10,000 active days:** This mitigates the risk that values "reach maximum saturation too quickly or show no observable changes."
- **Server-side tunable config records:** The plan says to keep variables "easily tunable" for drift calibration.

#### Audio Uncanniness

- **Tiny randomized variations in carrier frequency and envelope durations:** The plan uses jitter to mitigate synthesized calls sounding "electronic or artificial" and "to replicate muscle movement in real birds."

#### Screen-Reader Bloat

- **Strict 45-second debounce:** The plan uses this to prevent automated narration from flooding "screen reader queues," except during direct keyboard clicks.
