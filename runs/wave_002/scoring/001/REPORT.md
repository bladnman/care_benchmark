## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Both were strong. Affective recovery was slightly higher by weight (90/98) than functional recovery (46/54). Functional losses came mostly from evidence-bound layer compression in F1/F13 presence precision and F35 deletion privacy.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered at 98/108. The high-weight material generally survived because the plan encoded it as invariants, architecture, and tests. Weight-2 losses were concentrated in targeted headroom additions where the mechanism survived but the rationale did not, especially F29 and F37.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** Feature-level fidelity (112/124) was slightly higher than system-level fidelity (24/28). The reconstruction followed the plan's sections very closely, so specific implementation rationales survived. A few broad philosophies, especially S3 and S4, were present as rules but less explicitly elevated as system principles.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Missing layers were usually secondary or downstream rationale layers: F1/F13 compressed the population-scale silent-failure argument; F31 compressed the distinction between identity continuity and vector persistence; F35 compressed the privacy reason for hard deletion.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Audio, sync, accessibility, privacy, anti-gamification, and first-frame performance were especially faithful because the plan encoded them with invariants and tests. The weakest subdomain was social sharing nuance: F37's per-invite rule survived, but the "private relationship, not publishing" why did not.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The v06 evidence gate denied plausible v1-style credit for F29 and F37 because the frozen reconstruction had operational rules but not the gold rationale. It also held F1, F13, F31, and F35 to partial rather than full recovery because one layer was missing or compressed.

Overall, this is a high-carry plan: it preserves almost every build surface and much of the rationale through structural mechanisms. The remaining leakage is mostly in targeted headroom whys where a good implementation can still omit the deeper product reason.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep targeted feature-level headroom additions. F29 and F37 did useful work: the plan and reconstruction were excellent, but these two still separated rule survival from rationale survival.
- Keep the v06 evidence ledger. It prevented over-crediting mechanism-only rows and made partial multi-layer calls transparent.
- Consider splitting system-level scoring guidance between "system section only" and "whole reconstruction" evidence. This run's reconstruction preserved some system ideas in per-feature sections rather than the explicit system list, which made S3/S4/S6 judgment more subjective.
- Add a small calibration note for multi-layer partials: whether one missing layer in an otherwise exact reconstruction should always be 0.5 is coarse, but it is consistent and easy to audit.
- Preserve the product mix. Pocket Aviary generated a healthy split of affective and functional failures; privacy, accessibility, social, audio, and simulation all produced meaningful signal.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The assigned prompt stated fresh context and the frozen reconstruction was read-only. I did not modify it and found no contamination evidence.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run, so it has no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I counted all 120 features as captured. The only mild borderline was feature 25, starter-bird adoption, because the plan carried the starter/adoption flow more clearly than the no-catalog rationale. This did not affect fidelity reachability because the feature was included either way under the inclusive capture rule.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The cross-cutting bar was met for all S1-S9 in the plan. The main subjectivity was whether the reconstruction elevated S3, S4, and S6 enough at the system level; I scored those partial rather than full.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no ungrounded reconstruction that affected recovery. Where the reconstruction was thinner than the plan, I scored partial or none rather than inferring the missing rationale.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F29 and F37 were rule-without-why denials. F1, F13, F31, and F35 were partial because not all layers passed the reconstruction-evidence gate.
