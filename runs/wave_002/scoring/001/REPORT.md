## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better (34.0/54 = 63.0%) than affective whys (48.0/98 = 49.0%). The strongest functional preservation was architectural: S7, F18, F36. Affective losses clustered around social/privacy meanings that were implemented as rules but not explained, such as F23, F37, F38, and F39.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered at 51.9%, below weight-2 whys at 59.1%. The high-weight rows often had enough plan signal for one or two layers but lost the downstream consequence layer, as in F2, F4, F9, F10, F13, F16, F17, F25, and F30.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** The planner preserved philosophy very well: system-level fidelity was 85.7%. Feature-level fidelity was much lower at 46.8%, meaning the plan was a strong implementation compression but a leaky encoding of detailed intent.
- **Multi-layer recovery.** Layer 1 survived most often. Layer 2 survived when it was operationally explicit, such as calibration or multi-device sync. Layer 3 was the main loss: downstream product-collapse consequences were often absent or reduced to terse risk labels.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, sync, accessibility, and privacy telemetry did best because the plan contained concrete architecture. Social optionality and anti-gamification were captured as exclusions but often lost their relationship-level reasons.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Fourteen rows were rule-without-why cases. Three rows had plan-only rationale not fully reconstructed: F2's Tamagotchi/screensaver calibration band, F4's looped-audio/aliveness consequence, and F40's narration cadence/queue rationale.

The failure shape suggests a high-coverage planner that writes an executable build plan, but compresses affective guardrails into terse principle labels. That is excellent for building many features, weaker for preserving why certain tempting product patterns must never be added.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more targeted headroom rows for rule-only exclusions. This run implemented many refusals while dropping the reason: no social notifications, no public discovery, no editable notebook, and no show-off visit mode.
- Keep multi-layer scoring, but make downstream-consequence layers easier to evidence by requiring plans to include explicit "failure mode if wrong" bullets. The largest losses were not the main mechanisms; they were the consequences.
- Maintain the system-level cross-cutting bar. It discriminated well here: the plan clearly carried S6-S9 architecturally while still losing several feature-specific whys.
- For future Pocket Aviary-like instances, add a small set of social/privacy whys that distinguish "private relationship" from ordinary privacy compliance, since that distinction was repeatedly flattened.
- Tighten phase-2A reconstruction guidance to avoid overusing `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN` when the plan has exact rule evidence but not rationale; that distinction is useful and should be explicit.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The supplied frozen reconstruction appears to be from a separate phase-2A context and was not modified during scoring. This scorer did not read `prd/`, peer slots, or other waves.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run for candidate model `grok-4.5` at low effort; it has no variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Three inclusive capture calls were made: #57 evening palette shift, #64 top bar contents, and #97 visitor sees actual aviary. One narrow non-capture call was #52 because the plan had a 5s undo window but not click-anywhere undo. These calls do not affect feature-level why reachability because none are F1-F40 anchors except #97, which was included.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The 3-feature bar was easy to satisfy for all S rows. The subjective part was layer completeness for S1 and S2, where the core principles were clear but downstream affective consequences were compressed.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction cases were found. The reconstruction mostly quotes or paraphrases the plan.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several plausible v1-style recoveries were denied because only the rule survived: F5, F14, F19, F22, F23, F26, F27, F31, F33, F35, F37, F38, F39, and F40.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** Fourteen included whys were rule-only or nearly rule-only; this is the main diagnostic gap between planning quality and fidelity.
