## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 32/54; affective whys recovered 57/98. The feature-level affective exceptions were the leakiest area: F8, F29, F31, F33, F37, and F39 mostly survived as rules, not reasons.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys did better overall (72/108) because the plan made server authority, accessibility, and anti-announcement principles explicit. But many multi-layer rows recovered only L1 or L1+L2.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (89.3%) was much higher than feature-level fidelity (51.6%). The plan carried philosophy well; the reconstruction compressed detailed feature rationales.
- **Layer loss.** Layer 3 downstream consequences were commonly dropped: F2 lost the failure-mode endpoints, F18 lost the invisible deleted-drift scenario, F40 lost the screen-reader queue/announcement-monitoring consequence.
- **Subdomains.** Accessibility and server/sync were strongest. Social/privacy had good technical boundaries but often missed affective social rationales. Audio preserved procedural implementation but lost the anti-loop affective spine.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Rows such as F5, F8, F11, F19, F29, F31, F33, F34, F35, F37, and F39 would look acceptable as implementation plans, but v06 denies fidelity because the why is absent from the frozen reconstruction.

What this suggests: the planner wrote a strong implementation artifact, and the reconstructor honestly followed it, but implementation-level compression stripped many of the relationship-protecting whys that make Pocket Aviary distinctive.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. It prevented rule-only implementation details from being mistaken for recovered intent.
- Add a report-only binding-error flag. F6 attached the bird-cap area to age-gated anti-gamification rather than empirical call recognizability; that is not pure hallucination, but it is a wrong-feature why.
- Preserve targeted headroom additions. F29-F40 were useful because several were captured functionally while their deeper rationale disappeared.
- Consider asking reconstructors to preserve explicit "why" phrases separately from build rules. The current reconstruction was honest, but many sections summarize behavior first and rationale second, which makes downstream scoring stricter.
