## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better: 26/48 (54.2%) versus affective 27/80 (33.8%). Architecture and privacy boundaries were explicit; social and interaction feelings often collapsed into non-goal labels.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 42/100; weight-2 recovered 11/28. High-weight rows did not fully survive; they often kept L1 and dropped L2/L3.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (53.6%) exceeded feature-level fidelity (38.0%). The planner preserved broad philosophy more reliably than feature-specific rationales.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanisms survived most often: presence conjunction, low-pass drift, server tick, WebAudio calls, and accessibility cadence. Secondary temptation and downstream consequence layers were usually missing.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accessibility/performance and accounts/sync were strongest. Interactions, layout ambience, and social optionality had many missing or rule-only rows.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** F6, F9, F14, F16, F21, F23, F29, F31, F32, F33, and F37 show the main pattern: the plan/reconstruction preserved a rule, but not the why.

The failure shape suggests a strong implementation planner that captured buildable architecture while compressing affective product safeguards into ordinary requirements.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator; it caught many mechanism-only rows that would otherwise look adequate.
- Add more targeted exception whys around tempting additions: welcome toasts, visit badges, editable notebooks, show-off sharing, public comparison, and trait-stat transparency.
- Preserve the current functional/affective mix, but add examples for affective semantic-equivalence calls so scorers handle poetic rationales consistently.
- Add a report field distinguishing broad feature capture from detailed sub-rule capture. One-line captures such as return-greeting should remain reachable but visibly compressed.
- Clarify when plan-only cross-cutting earns partial system-level recovery, especially for restraint, specificity, and accessibility.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The scoring followed the fresh-context phase 2B workflow and did not modify the frozen reconstruction.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one shot; there is no variance signal inside this slot.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive for compressed anchors such as starter birds, return-greeting, mood persistence, and scene loads with motion. These calls increased the reachable feature denominator more than they helped the numerator.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The strict three-feature bar was most subjective for S3 and S4. The appendix above lists the evidence trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction cases were found; the reconstruction mostly stayed plan-derived.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Rows with only operational rules were denied why credit even when the product rule was present.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** There were 12 included rule-without-why cases, concentrated in bird identity, social sharing, no gamification, and notebook constraints.
