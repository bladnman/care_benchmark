## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered slightly better (32.0/54.0) than affective whys (52.0/98.0). Server canonicality (S7/F17), privacy boundaries (S8/F36), deletion (F35), and accessibility architecture (S9/F24/F25) survived clearly. Affective product-relationship rationales leaked more often: F8, F11, F22, F29, F38, F39, and F40.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered better than weight-2 whys (64.0/108.0 vs 20.0/44.0), mostly because the plan and reconstruction emphasized load-bearing architecture and launch gates. However, many weight-3 rows were partial rather than full because B preserved only the primary mechanism.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was higher (20.0/28.0) than feature-level fidelity (64.0/124.0). The reconstruction was good at naming broad architecture and accessibility/privacy posture, but often converted feature-level rationale into build mechanics.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Downstream-consequence layers were most often lost. F2 kept low-pass drift but lost the Tamagotchi-vs-screensaver band; F10 kept no welcome text but lost the reframing consequence; F18 kept additive deltas but lost the invisible lost-drift failure scenario.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation/sync and accessibility were strongest. Social/visit whys were weakest: the plan captured visits, logs, exact visitor view, and no leaderboards, but B mostly reconstructed rules rather than why comparison, ambient exposure, and attention loops are product-shaping risks.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Fifteen rows were rule-without-why cases. Six rows had plan-only rationale not reconstructed. No row was penalized for ungrounded reconstruction.

The failure shape suggests a high-capacity planner whose plan encoded nearly all requirements, followed by a reconstruction that favored operational architecture over affective intent.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep evidence-bound scoring. This run would look much stronger under a mechanism-only read; v06 exposed the difference between preserving what to build and preserving why the product needs that shape.
- Add more targeted affective exception whys. The largest losses were around refusal surfaces: no optimization, no announcement, no comparison, no show-off mode, no attention loops.
- Keep multi-layer whys and continue emphasizing downstream consequences. The third layer was often the discriminator between good implementation recall and genuine intent recovery.
- Consider a separate headline-adjacent diagnostic for rule-without-why. In this run, 15 such cases explain why 100% planning capture still produced only 55.3% fidelity.
- Preserve the system-level cross-cutting appendix. It made S3 and S4 transparent: the plan preserved them, but the reconstruction did not identify them as principles.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This phase-2B pass used only the allowed phase-two files, run 001 plan/metadata/timing, and the frozen reconstruction. The reconstruction was not modified.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one candidate plan and one reconstruction. No variance signal is available from this single slot.
- **Borderline capture calls.** All 120 features were counted captured. Documentation-like anchors such as the product concept statement and glossary were credited because the plan carried their substance throughout the implementation plan.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The plan met the three-place inheritance bar for all S1-S9, but S3 and S4 scored only partial because the reconstruction did not identify those principles.
- **Confabulation cases.** None were scored. Some reconstruction language was compressed, but I did not find recovered claims that were ungrounded in the plan.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F1, F13, F23, and F40 had plan-side grounding but no reconstruction-side why evidence. F33 was explicitly marked NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN and scored none.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** Fifteen included feature whys preserved the operational rule without the gold rationale. These counted for capture but not for recovery.
- **Operational timing.** TIMING.json supplied phase1 and phase2a for run 001 but no phase2b timing at scoring-write time, so the score JSON includes only bounded available phase timing.
