## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 40.0 / 54.0 weighted points (74.1%). Affective whys recovered 77.0 / 98.0 (78.6%). The difference is small; the more important pattern is exception-whys versus architecture-whys.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered better: 88.0 / 108.0 (81.5%) versus 29.0 / 44.0 (65.9%) for weight-2. The multi-layer rows gave credit for partially surviving rationale, while single-layer rows such as F5, F19, F23, F29, F33, F37, and F39 had no partial-credit path.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System principles survived strongly (25.0 / 28.0). Feature-level fidelity was lower (92.0 / 124.0) because the reconstruction often turned highly specific social/privacy/charm exceptions into rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes usually survived. Dropped layers were most often secondary/downstream: F1/F13 lost silent population-wide corruption, F16 lost retrofit risk, F30 lost economy/unlock erosion, and F31 lost retroactive relationship loss.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Audio, accessibility, server authority, and privacy were particularly strong. Social sharing and notebook/account-edge rationales lost more: F23, F33, F37, and F39 were mechanism-only.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter operator mattered. A looser scorer might have credited many rule-preserving rows, but v06 denied recovery where the frozen reconstruction did not carry the why.

What the failure shape suggests: the candidate plan is an unusually complete build plan, and the encoding bottleneck is not feature coverage. It is the survival of compact, non-obvious product rationale when a downstream agent reconstructs from a dense implementation plan.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** I treated the reconstruction as frozen and did not modify it. The validity audit found no gold ID leakage and no gold-list heading mirror.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one plan/reconstruction/scoring path; it has no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Feature 7, the glossary row, was counted as captured because domain terms are defined operationally throughout the plan, though not as a literal glossary. This does not affect feature-level fidelity because it is not a gold-why-bearing row.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 and S6 were the fine calls. PLAN preserved both cross-cuttingly, but the reconstruction was less explicit than the gold why for restraint and for the population-level precision of presence.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no scored ungrounded reconstruction cases. Some rationale was thin, but it traced to the PLAN rather than invented content.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Rule-only rows denied recovery: F5, F19, F23, F29, F33, F35, F37, and F39. F35 was explicitly marked NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN, so it scores none.
- **Operational timing.** TIMING.json had phase1 and phase2a data for run 001; phase2b timing was not present at score-write time, so the score JSON includes only the bounded phase fields available.
