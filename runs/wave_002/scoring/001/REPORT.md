## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** The split was close: functional whys recovered 42.0/54.0 (77.8%) and affective whys 75.0/98.0 (76.5%). The affective losses were concentrated in relationship-register subtleties: F11, F29, F39, and partials like S2, F10, F14, F27, and F30.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-2 whys recovered 35.0/44.0 (79.5%) and weight-3 whys recovered 82.0/108.0 (75.9%). Multi-layer targets exposed compression: several primary rules survived while downstream consequences were absent.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 82.1%, higher than feature-level fidelity at 75.8%. The plan preserved philosophy strongly, but the reconstruction compressed some targeted feature rationales into implementation rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** The most common lost layer was the downstream consequence or secondary contributor: examples include F1/F13 silent calibration failure, F4 chorus-loop artifact, F10 all textual welcome variants, F16 retrofit consequence, and F40 screen-reader queue overload.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility were strong where the plan had architectural rationale. Layout missed three planning details in the 120-row denominator, and social had one feature-why miss: F39 kept same-state visiting but lost the anti-show-off rationale.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** v06 denied credit to mechanism-only rows: F5 mood-shaped idle motion, F11 settle optionality, F29 starter birds not catalog choices, and F39 visitor actual-aviary rendering. These all counted for planning capture but not intent recovery.

Overall failure shape: the candidate plan was nearly complete and unusually implementation-ready, but its compression style favored durable engineering rules over some of the PRD's more poetic relationship rationale. The benchmark still had headroom because targeted why rows asked not just "what should be built" but "why this rule protects the product."

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the v06 evidence-bound operator. It cleanly separated high planning coverage from lower rationale recovery, especially for F29-F40 targeted additions.
- Add a small set of targeted planning features around low-salience UI/account details. This run missed evening palette/call quieting, parallax, and the privacy-policy link despite capturing almost everything else.
- Clarify scoring guidance for single-layer system whys that are partly reconstructed as a neighboring subprinciple. S4 exposed this: B recovered sparsity but not the full depth-per-bird over visual/product richness rationale.
- Preserve multi-layer whys for calibration-sensitive engineering choices. Presence, drift, server tick, LWW, and narration cadence all produced meaningful partial/full distinctions.
- Consider more headroom whys in social/share surfaces. F39 showed that a plan can build the correct visitor state while omitting the "not a marketing rendering" relationship reason.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** Scoring used the frozen reconstruction, assigned plan, phase-two scoring packet, candidate metadata, and only `runs.001` timing. No PRD files, peer slots, or other waves were read.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run with no variance signal. The score should be interpreted as a point estimate for this candidate/harness/effort combination.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Background-tab pause was counted as captured because visible-only polling, visibility resume, and hidden-tab no-drift were explicit. Evening palette was not counted because broad day/night and settle dimming did not specify warmer evening hues plus quieter calls.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The strict three-feature inheritance bar was applied. S4 was the most subjective system-level call: the plan preserved restraint, while B reconstructed only a narrower sparsity principle.
- **Confabulation cases.** No scored recovery was marked ungrounded. The validity audit found no clear contamination or confabulated gold mapping.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F5, F11, F29, and F39 were mechanism-only in the reconstruction and therefore scored none despite feature capture.
- **Timing.** `TIMING.json` contained phase 1 and phase 2A entries for run 001 only. No phase 2B timing was present in the ledger at write time.
