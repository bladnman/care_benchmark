## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better: 32.0/54 (59.3%) versus 40.0/90 (44.4%) for affective whys. Server-canonical architecture and synthetic-ID privacy transferred cleanly; relationship rules such as no welcome toast, no streak counter, stable identity, and visitor actualness leaked.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** High-weight whys did better: 58.0/104 (55.8%) versus 14.0/40 (35.0%). Primary mechanism layers often survived while downstream consequence layers were compressed away.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 71.4%, above feature-level 44.8%. Broad architecture and product posture survived better than feature-local exceptions.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary cause layers were usually recovered; secondary and downstream layers were first to disappear, especially for affective whys like F14, F27, F31, and F39.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync was strongest. Accessibility was mixed: F25 full, F24/F40 partial. Social was mixed: F21/F37 survived, while F38/F39 rationale did not. Greeting/arrival details were weak at planning time.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** F8, F14, F22, F23, F26, F31-F35, F38, and F39 preserve rules or endpoints but not enough rationale evidence for recovery. No clear ungrounded reconstruction cases were found.

What this suggests: the candidate is strong at a shippable architecture and good at explicit system constraints, but it compresses subtle affective intent into implementation rules. The benchmark discriminates at the feature-rationale layer, not just feature enumeration.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** I treated this as a fresh phase-2B scoring context and did not use memory, peer slots, PRD, or other waves. The phase-2A reconstruction remained frozen and unmodified.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run, so there is no variance signal. Compare against wave aggregates, not in isolation.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I counted #25 starter birds, #51 background-tab pause, and #64 top-bar contents inclusively. These calls do not change the main fidelity finding.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S1, S2, S4, S5, and S6 were the most subjective. The cross-cutting appendix records the inheritance trail.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no material ungrounded reconstruction claims. The reconstruction is conservative and often says NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Mechanisms survived for numeric-vector hiding, no streaks, export/deletion, visit log, and actual visitor snapshot, but their gold rationales did not.
- **Operational compromise.** TIMING.json contained phase1 and phase2a timing only for slot 001. The score JSON includes those bounded fields and omits phase2b timing rather than fabricating it.
