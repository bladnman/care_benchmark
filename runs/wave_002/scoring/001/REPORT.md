## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 36/54 (66.7%). Affective whys recovered 60/94 (63.8%). Functional architecture was stronger: server ownership, privacy, event logs, and telemetry isolation were crisp. Affective exceptions leaked more often: F8, F23, F29, F37, F38, and F39 survived mostly as rules.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 74/108; weight-2 whys recovered 22/40. High-weight whys did better because many had concrete mechanisms in the plan, but their secondary/downstream layers were often compressed.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (85.7%) was much stronger than feature-level fidelity (60.0%). The planner preserved product philosophy broadly, then lost narrower rationales behind individual exceptions.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes survived most often. Secondary calibration layers survived when they were numerical or architectural (F2, F17), but downstream consequence layers were frequently dropped (F3, F10, F14, F18, F30, F35).
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, sync, privacy, audio, and accessibility were strong. Interaction nuance and social surfaces were weaker: return-greeting variation, settle opt-in, sync-conflict tone, no show-off sharing, and visit-log rationale all lost detail.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The strict evidence gate denied plausible v1-style credit for F6, F8, F23, F26, F29, F37, F38, and F39 because the reconstruction or plan carried the operational rule without the why.

What this suggests: the phase-1 plan is build-complete but rationale-compressed. It would likely produce a capable implementation, but future maintainers would not reliably inherit why several tempting product extensions are dangerous.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more targeted exception whys for social/onboarding/privacy-adjacent surfaces. This run implemented per-invite sharing and actual-aviary visits, but did not carry the private-relationship rationale.
- Keep the v06 evidence-bound operator. It made the main split clear: **102/120 planning capture** but only **72/120 feature-level recovered weight**.
- Add benchmark features whose operational rule is easy but whose rationale is subtle: greeting absence/boldness variation, settle-vs-close equivalence, sync-conflict tone, color/panning restraint, and browser support.
- Consider making downstream consequence layers more explicit in scoring reports. The most common partial pattern here was L1/L2 recovered with L3 missing.
- Preserve the system cross-cutting bar, but require scorers to name when a system why is plan-only. S4 showed how a plan can preserve restraint while the reconstruction fails to identify it.
