## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 26.0/46; affective whys recovered 13.0/64. The candidate preserved architecture much better than relationship tone.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** High-weight whys recovered 34.0/88; weight-2 whys recovered 5.0/22. Multi-layer rows often got only first-layer mechanics.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (46.4%) exceeded feature-level fidelity (31.7%). Philosophy survived when it was architectural, but feature-specific affective exceptions leaked quickly.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** The downstream-consequence layer was the most frequently dropped. F2 was the clearest full recovery; most other partials captured implementation cause without the failure-mode consequence.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and drift calibration were strongest. Return greetings, loading state, field-notebook read-only semantics, account export/deletion, visit log, visitor actual-aviary rendering, and system voice exceptions were missing.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Rule-without-why rows were common: F4, F12, F14, F21, F23, F26, F27, F31, F32, F36, F37, F40, plus S4/S5. These would be easy to over-credit without the v06 evidence gate.

What this suggests: the phase-1 plan is an implementable architecture sketch, not a durable intent carrier. It captures many nouns and mechanisms, but it frequently drops the reasons that prevent adjacent-but-wrong products.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more targeted affective exceptions like F29-F40; they exposed headroom where broad plans preserve a rule but not the product relationship.
- Keep the evidence-bound operator. It correctly denied credit to rule-only rows that would have inflated v1-style semantic scoring.
- Consider making named exclusions a larger slice of the gold surface. Missing no-toast, no-show-off mode, no visit-log notification, and matter-of-fact error tone changed the product shape.
- Preserve multi-layer whys, especially downstream consequences; they separated true intent carry-through from plausible implementation summaries.
- Add scorer examples for compressed cross-cutting plans. S3 and S6 required judgment because nearby plan decisions existed without the explicit principle.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The validity audit found no gold-ID leakage or gold-order mirroring; reconstruction appears plan-derived.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run, so there is no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** Inclusive calls affected several rows: 2, 15, 21, 24, 29, 53, 62, 85, 89, 90, 92, 93, and 104. Tightening those would lower planning quality modestly.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The strict three-feature bar was clearest for S7/S8/S9 and hardest for S3/S6.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction cases were counted for score aggregation.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** The biggest denial class was rule-without-why, not plan-only or ungrounded reconstruction.
