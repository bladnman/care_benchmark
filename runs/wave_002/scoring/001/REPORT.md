## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered much better by weight (23/44 = 52.3%) than affective whys (27/86 = 31.4%). Server state, sync, telemetry, and performance rationales had firmer plan hooks; relationship-shaping rules such as F23, F29, F31, F33, F37, and F39 mostly survived only as mechanisms.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 38/96 = 39.6%, modestly above weight-2 whys at 12/34 = 35.3%. The high-weight system whys helped, but many high-weight feature whys lost layers.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity (64.3%) was much stronger than feature-level fidelity (31.4%). The plan preserved broad architectural philosophy better than the feature-specific why trail.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanism layers often survived; secondary temptation/failure-mode layers were usually dropped. Examples: F2 keeps slow drift but loses one-week/three-week calibration; F17 keeps server tick and sync coherence but loses the client-tick collapse explanation.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accessibility and performance captured many features, but accessibility rationale still compressed. Accounts/sync recovered architecture strongly while missing account export/deletion and sync-tone surfaces. Social features were mechanism-heavy and why-light.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The v06 evidence gate denied likely v1-style credit for many rules: F4 procedural calls, F8 hidden vectors, F21 read-only visits, F23 no leaderboards, F31 stable identity, F33 read-only notebook, and F39 actual visitor view.

What the failure shape suggests: this plan is a competent build plan, but it often encodes product philosophy as implementation nouns instead of as decision-protecting rationale. The reconstruction stayed honest when the plan was thin, which depresses fidelity but preserves measurement validity.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound ledger. It sharply distinguishes buildable feature capture from why recovery, especially for F4, F8, F21, F23, F31, F33, and F39.
- Add more targeted headroom around social/privacy features. This run captured many social mechanisms but missed visit notification, visit log, sharing rationale, and show-off-mode rationale.
- Preserve multi-layer whys for high-risk features, but consider naming the expected layer categories in scoring tools. Most losses were secondary-contributor and downstream-consequence drops, not primary-rule drops.
- Keep functional and affective balance visible. Functional architecture scored better here; affective relationship-protection whys provided most discrimination.
- Revisit capture guidance for broad non-goal phrases. The plan specifically named streaks and social-network surfaces, so I counted those captures, but generic exclusions can hide missing variants like days-visited calendars and visit notifications.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** I treated phase 2A as frozen and did not modify `RECONSTRUCTION.md`. The reconstruction itself reads plan-derived and receives a PASS in the validity audit.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one scoring of one candidate plan; no variance signal is available inside this slot.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on several captures: starter adoption flow (#25), settle gesture (#43), no streak counter (#50), per-bird call signature (#18), social no-chat/profile surfaces (#93), and visitor actual-state view (#97). These raised planning quality but did not automatically raise fidelity.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The subjective calls were S1, S3, S4, S8, and S9. I required at least three inherited plan decisions and downgraded rows where the reconstruction named only a neighboring principle.
- **Confabulation cases.** I did not identify material ungrounded reconstruction assertions; most low scores came from honest non-recovery or mechanism-only recovery.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Many rows had enough rule evidence to build something plausible but not enough why evidence to score recovery.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** There were 20 included rule-without-why cases, concentrated in feature-level rows where the plan named the mechanism but not the relationship or failure-mode rationale.
