## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 27.0/44.0 weighted points (61.4%), while affective whys recovered 24.0/78.0 (30.8%). Server-side simulation, additive deltas, low-pass drift, and telemetry boundaries survived much better than relationship-protecting affective rationales.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 40.0/92.0 (43.5%); weight-2 whys recovered 11.0/30.0 (36.7%). Higher-weight rows did somewhat better, mostly because architecture-heavy rows were explicit in the plan.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 53.6%, higher than feature-level fidelity at 38.3%. The reconstruction inferred broad architecture and restraint but lost many feature-level exception rationales.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary mechanisms were often recovered, secondary rationales sometimes, downstream consequences rarely. Lost layer-3 examples include S1, F7, F15, F17, F24, and F25.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation and sync were strongest. Social, notebook, greeting, and anti-announcement surfaces were weakest. Accessibility mechanics were captured, but accessibility-as-equal-affective-quality was mostly compressed away.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The strict evidence gate denied credit for plausible but mechanism-only rows such as F5, F8, F9, F14, F21, F23, F27, F31, F37, and F39.

What the failure shape suggests: this candidate is competent at extracting implementable architecture from the PRD, but it compresses product philosophy into feature labels. It keeps the what, loses much of the why.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound v06 ledger. It made mechanism-only preservation visible and prevented accidental credit inflation.
- Add or retain more targeted feature-level exceptions around social privacy, no-announcement surfaces, and accessibility quality. Those were the best discriminators in this run.
- Preserve multi-layer whys, especially downstream-consequence layers. The candidate frequently recovered primary mechanisms while dropping consequences.
- Consider more direct probes for "notice, never announce" beyond no-toast, because this run missed S2 despite capturing adjacent no-gamification rules.
- Keep functional and affective whys mixed. The functional rows checked architecture competence; the affective rows checked whether the planner carried the product relationship logic.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** This scorer used only the allowed phase-two, plan, timing, scoring, and frozen reconstruction paths. The frozen reconstruction was not modified.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one slot in wave_002 and should not be treated as variance-stable.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive where the implementation rule was present but the rationale or precise PRD detail was thin. The main borderline captures were features 9, 18, 24, 29, 32, 63, 85, 92, 97, and 103.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The three-inheritance bar was strictest for S3, S5, S6, and S8.
- **Confabulation cases.** I found no clear ungrounded reconstruction assertions; the main issue was omission, not invented rationale.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several rows that would look plausible under loose semantic scoring were denied because the reconstruction did not carry the gold rationale.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** The report marks 17 included rows as rule_without_why. These are the core diagnostic of this run.
