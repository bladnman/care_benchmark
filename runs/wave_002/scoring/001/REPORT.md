## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 30/54; affective whys recovered 61/98. The affective losses cluster around relationship-shaping rationales: F8, F10, F22, F23, F29, F33, and F34.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 66/108; weight-2 whys recovered 25/44. The heavy rows often preserved primary causes but lost downstream consequences.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 82.1%, while feature-level fidelity was 54.8%. Broad architecture and philosophy survived better than local exception rationales.
- **Multi-layer patterns.** L1 primary causes were most often recovered. L2/L3 losses show up in F1, F2, F7, F16, F17, F18, F20, F30, and F31.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accessibility and first-load performance scored strongly. Social/account lifecycle features more often preserved what to build while losing why it mattered.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Eleven rows were rule-without-why, and F10 was plan-only-not-reconstructed. No ungrounded reconstruction was counted.

What this suggests: the candidate plan is an excellent implementation scaffold, but its rationale encoding is uneven. The frozen reconstruction can infer many engineering whys from the plan, but it does not consistently preserve the product's more delicate relationship and anti-gamification exceptions.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the v06 evidence-bound operator. It made the high planning score and lower feature-rationale score visible instead of collapsing them into one impression.
- Add or retain targeted headroom around social/privacy/account-lifecycle exceptions. F22, F23, F34, and F35 exposed mechanism-only planning.
- Encourage phase-1 plans to attach exception rationales directly to the feature, not just state broad principles once. F8, F19, and F33 are the clearest examples.
- Continue using the system cross-cutting appendix. S4 and S6 show why the (b) judgment needs an explicit trail.

- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 and S6 were the subjective cases: PLAN preserved the principles, but B did not identify them cleanly in system intent.
- **Confabulation cases.** None were counted. The reconstruction stayed grounded in plan language.
