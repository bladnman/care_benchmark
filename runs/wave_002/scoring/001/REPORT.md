## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 44.0 / 54.0 (81.5%), while affective whys recovered 55.0 / 98.0 (56.1%). Functional architecture around presence, server ticks, no LWW, and telemetry survived best. Affective social and relationship-protection rationales leaked most.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 76.0 / 108.0 (70.4%); weight-2 whys recovered 23.0 / 44.0 (52.3%). Multi-layer rows often got the first layer but lost temptation/cascade layers.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was 89.3%, much higher than feature-level fidelity at 59.7%. The planner preserved product philosophy cross-cuttingly, while the reconstruction compressed many local exceptions into rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary layers were usually recovered; secondary and downstream consequence layers were the common losses. Examples: F10 lost the well-meaning-toast temptation and adjacent-surface cascade; F20 lost the relationship-as-data-product layer; F25 lost the calls/drift/notebook-still-alive layer.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, sync, presence, and performance were strong. Social optional features and targeted relationship-copy/account lifecycle whys were weak. Accessibility was mixed: narration cadence was strong, but reduced-motion rationale was compressed.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Twelve included rows were rule-without-why cases. There were no ungrounded reconstruction cases; the stricter v06 gate mainly denied credit where the frozen reconstruction honestly said NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN or repeated a mechanism without rationale.

What the failure shape suggests: this candidate was excellent at enumerating and engineering product behaviors, especially server/presence/privacy architecture, but it often compressed the human reason for negative requirements. The benchmark therefore still has discrimination despite high planning coverage.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the targeted F29-F40 headroom additions. They prevented this otherwise comprehensive plan from saturating feature-level fidelity.
- Add or preserve more local exception whys in future PRD instances, especially for social sharing, export/deletion, and user-authored vs observer-authored surfaces.
- Retain the evidence-bound operator. It cleanly separated feature capture from intent recovery and exposed rule-only reconstruction.
- Clarify scoring guidance for system-level multi-layer whys whose downstream layer is launch/process timing, as with S9.
- Consider requiring planners to include compact rationale annotations for negative requirements, because most misses were prohibitions implemented without their reason.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The phase setup states fresh context, and I did not spawn subagents or background agents. No same-context contamination was observed.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one run only, with no variance signal.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive for features 29, 31, 32, 76, 90, 94, and 97. These calls raise planning quality but do not inflate recovery without why evidence.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 was the main subjective call: the plan preserved restraint across cap, scene layout, and audio recognizability, but B did not name the system principle.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction cases were scored; the reconstruction reads plan-derived.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F8, F22, F23, F29-F34, and F37-F39 were denied or mostly denied because the frozen reconstruction lacked the gold rationale.
- **Operational compromise.** TIMING.json contained phase 1 and phase 2A only; phase 2B timing was omitted rather than fabricated.
