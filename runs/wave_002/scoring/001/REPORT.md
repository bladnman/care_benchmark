## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered slightly better by weight (42/54 = 77.8%) than affective whys (73/98 = 74.5%). The strongest functional recoveries were server ownership, privacy telemetry boundaries, and performance. Affective leakage clustered in social/engagement exceptions: F22, F23, F29, F30, and F37 kept rules but not the deeper relationship rationale.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys did better (84/108 = 77.8%) than weight-2 whys (31/44 = 70.5%), mainly because the plan and reconstruction repeatedly surfaced the load-bearing architecture: drift, presence, server tick, accessibility, and first-frame aliveness. Weight-2 losses were often compact single-layer rationales where a missing phrase meant no partial credit.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level intent was almost intact (27/28 = 96.4%). Feature-level fidelity fell to 88/124 = 71.0% because the reconstruction often summarized feature rules without carrying the local why. S4 was the only system-level partial: the plan preserved restraint, but B did not name it as a system principle.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** The primary layer was usually easiest to recover; downstream consequences were dropped most often. Feature-level misses in F1/F13, F4, F16/F18, F27, F31, and F35 tended to preserve mechanism while omitting the failure mode or relationship consequence.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Engine/sync/privacy/accessibility were strongest: F2, F3, F15, F17, F20, F24, F25, F32, F36, and F40 were full. Social and onboarding were weakest: F22, F23, F29, F30, and F37 were rule-only or none.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** Seven cases were explicit `rule_without_why`: F5, F19, F22, F23, F29, F30, and F37. Four more were plan-only or plan-richer than reconstruction in material layers: S4, F1, F4, and F13. The stricter v06 evidence gate meaningfully reduced what a looser semantic scorer might have credited.

The failure shape suggests this candidate is excellent at exhaustive implementation planning and at preserving cross-cutting architecture, but it compresses some small affective exceptions into product rules. That is exactly the kind of headroom the F29-F40 additions were meant to expose.

---

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound split between feature capture and why recovery. This run would otherwise look nearly saturated at planning quality, but the feature-level why ledger still exposed meaningful losses.
- Add more targeted single-feature affective exceptions in social/onboarding/account surfaces. F29, F30, and F37 show that frontier plans can implement the rule while dropping the relationship premise.
- Consider making selected weight-2 affective whys multi-layer when they contain two separable ideas. F19 and F22 were all-or-nothing single-layer losses even though they partially preserved the broader system principle.
- Preserve the current system-level cross-cutting bar, but add a clearer check for S4-style restraint. Restraint can be implemented everywhere yet omitted from the reconstructor's system-level summary.
- Keep denominator exclusion separate from recovery. This plan captured all feature anchors, so the full feature fidelity denominator was reachable; that made feature-level leakage visible instead of hidden behind planning misses.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The prompt specified fresh context and no prior-run memory; I did not use memory, subagents, browser search, PRD files, peer slots, or other waves. Phase 2A reconstruction remained frozen and unmodified.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one candidate plan and one reconstruction; no variance signal is available from this slot alone.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I marked features 90 and 93 borderline but captured. The per-invite/read-only visitor model and social-network exclusions semantically cover default-off visits and no chat/comment/avatar surfaces. If both were scored missed, planning would be 118/120 instead of 120/120.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** S4 is the fine call: the PLAN preserved restraint across bird cap, one-screen layout, sparse chrome, and audio recognizability, but B did not identify the principle in its system-level section.
- **Confabulation cases.** No clear ungrounded reconstruction cases were found. The validity audit separately records no contamination signatures.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** Several apparent recoveries were denied or reduced because reconstruction evidence did not carry the gold rationale or all layers, especially F1, F4, F13, F16, F18, F27, F31, and F35.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** F5, F19, F22, F23, F29, F30, and F37 preserved buildable rules but did not reconstruct the local why with enough specificity to earn feature-level recovery.
