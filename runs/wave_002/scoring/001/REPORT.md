## 3. Diagnostic patterns

**Headline shape.** Planning capture was near complete (116/120) and every F1-F40 anchor was reachable. The fidelity loss came after capture: the reconstruction preserved the system philosophy fully, then lost feature-level depth where the plan had rules without the gold rationale.


**Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered 44.0/54.0 weighted points; affective whys recovered 84.0/98.0. The affective side was not globally weaker, but the misses were characteristically affective: F5 mood motion, F19 error-tone clarity, and F39 actual-aviary sharing kept the rule while dropping the relationship reason.


**Multi-layer pattern.** Primary causes survived best. Secondary or downstream layers were trimmed in F1, F7, F10, F16, F18, F27, F31, and F35, usually where the plan said what to build and one immediate reason, but did not retain the failure-mode story.


**Subdomain pattern.** Accessibility, telemetry/privacy, audio, and server simulation scored strongly. The weaker areas were small literal feature coverage (species-pool count, nightjar night state, parallax, privacy-policy link) and rationale for social/voice exceptions.


**Evidence-bound effect.** The stricter v06 gate mattered. F5, F19, and F39 would look implemented in a build checklist, but were scored none because their gold whys were not in the frozen reconstruction and the plan grounding was mechanism-level.


## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep v06 evidence binding. This run shows why: implementation-level capture was saturated, but the evidence gate still separated rule survival from why survival.
- Add explicit rationale prompts for exception features. Several losses were exceptions whose rule was obvious but whose reason was not, especially F19 and F39.
- Retain small literal requirements in a checklist. The only planning misses were species-pool size, nightjar night state, parallax, and privacy-policy link.
- Preserve downstream consequence layers. Weight-3 losses often retained L1 but compressed L2/L3. Future planners should carry at least one failure-mode sentence per high-risk feature.
- Keep the system-level cross-cutting bar. It was easy to apply here because the plan had repeated concrete inheritance; the feature-level layer gate did the finer discrimination.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** The phase-2A reconstruction was treated as frozen and plan-derived; no reconstruction edits were made. The validity audit found no significant contamination signs.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one shot. No variance signal in a single run.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on #7 glossary, #24 naming at adoption, and #57 evening palette/call quieting. These calls do not affect feature-level reachability because none is an F1-F40 anchor.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** All S1-S9 met the strict three-inheritance bar in the plan.
- **Confabulation cases.** No ungrounded reconstruction was credited.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** F5, F19, and F39 were denied recovery because the rule survived without the gold why.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** F5, F19, and F39 are the mechanism-only cases counted in the evidence audit.
