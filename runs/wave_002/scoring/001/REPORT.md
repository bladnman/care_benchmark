## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional:** Functional whys recovered 28.0/54.0; affective whys recovered 38.0/94.0. The candidate preserved architecture/privacy whys better than relationship-shaping affect.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2:** Weight-3 whys recovered 52.0/108.0, while weight-2 whys recovered 14.0/40.0. High-weight items survived somewhat better because they were more often named in the plan.
- **System-level vs feature-level:** The system split is the main story: broad principles such as S7, S8, and S9 survived, but feature-level whys often collapsed into implementation rules.
- **Multi-layer recovery:** Primary mechanisms frequently survived; downstream consequences were the common loss. F2, F15, F30, and F40 are representative.
- **Subdomain patterns:** Accounts/sync and accessibility were strongest. Layout micro-details, social optional rationale, and interaction exceptions leaked most.
- **Evidence-bound effects:** F5, F6, F8, F19, F26, F31, F34, F37, F38, and F39 would look better under looser semantic scoring, but v06 correctly treats them as rule-without-why.

What this suggests: the planner encoded a buildable product with strong broad architecture but compressed many affective rationales into checklist mechanisms. The reconstructor was mostly honest about that compression.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the v06 evidence gate. It exposes the central difference between mechanism capture and why recovery.
- Preserve targeted social/privacy headroom additions. F37-F39 and F21-F23 were especially useful at finding rule-only planning.
- Keep multi-layer scoring for high-value whys, and consider emphasizing the downstream-consequence layer in scorer UI because it was most often dropped.
- Add a small exactness flag for features like presence where a phrase such as "precise 3-signal definition" is not enough to rebuild the exact rule.
- Consider more affective exception whys in future products; this run shows affective whys discriminate more sharply than straightforward architecture whys.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity:** The reconstruction reads plan-derived; the separate validity audit verdict is PASS. I did not modify `RECONSTRUCTION.md`.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation:** This is a single run without variance.
- **Borderline capture calls:** I leaned inclusive on presence definition, no welcome-back toast, per-bird call signature, background-tab behavior, visits-default-off, and no chat/comments/avatars. These affected planning quality more than fidelity.
- **System-level cross-cutting:** S3, S5, and S6 are the subjective cases. PLAN preserved several rules, but the reconstruction did not name the full system why.
- **Confabulation cases:** I found no material ungrounded reconstruction assertions. The reconstruction usually stayed close to PLAN and often said `NOT RECOVERABLE FROM PLAN` when appropriate.
- **Evidence-bound denials:** Many apparent recoveries were denied because either the reconstruction or plan only preserved a rule. This is most visible in F5, F6, F8, F19, F26, F31, F34, and F39.
- **Operational compromise:** `PLAN.md` appears truncated at its final phase-gate bullet (`zero gamificati`), but all needed sections before that were readable. `TIMING.json` supplied phase 1 and phase 2A timing only; no phase 2B duration was fabricated.
