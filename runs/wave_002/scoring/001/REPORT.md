## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys survived better: 34.0 / 54.0 weighted points (63.0%) vs. affective 50.0 / 98.0 (51.0%). Server-canonical sync (S7/F17) and privacy/telemetry boundaries (S8/F36) survived better than welcome, social, and relationship-protection whys (F10, F23, F39).
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Weight-3 whys recovered 56.0 / 108.0 (51.9%), below weight-2 whys at 28.0 / 44.0 (63.6%). Multi-layer rows often kept the primary rule while dropping secondary/downstream consequences.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level recovery (71.4%) was much stronger than feature-level recovery (51.6%). The reconstruction inferred broad architecture and product stance better than per-feature exceptions.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Layer 1 survived most often. Layer 3 was commonly lost: F1/F13 lost silent drift corruption, F3 lost the quieter-not-mistrust consequence, F30 lost economy collapse, and F40 lost announcement-style monitoring.
- **Subdomain patterns.** Accounts/sync and accessibility were strongest. Return greeting, welcome surfaces, social comparison/show-off, and starter/adoption rationale were weakest.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The stricter operator denied credit for rule-only rows such as F8, F11, F14, F19, F23, F31, F39, and F40.

The failure shape suggests a high-coverage implementation plan whose operational details are better preserved than its affective and exception-level rationale.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the targeted headroom whys (F29-F40). They exposed meaningful gaps even in a strong plan.
- Add or sharpen social/welcome exception whys. The planner captured no-public-social rules, but the reconstruction dropped why comparison and show-off modes are product-changing.
- Preserve multi-layer judging and consider asking reconstructors to separate primary, secondary, and downstream rationales without gold IDs. The dominant loss pattern is layer 3.
- Keep evidence-bound scoring. It prevented mechanism-only preservation from inflating why recovery.
- Consider adding a small explicit "rule-only" diagnostic score in future reports. It would make the difference between implementation coverage and intent coverage easier to compare.
