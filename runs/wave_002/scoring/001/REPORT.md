## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Affective vs functional.** Functional whys recovered better by weight (28.0/42.0, 66.7%) than affective whys (56.0/106.0, 52.8%). The plan and reconstruction were strongest where architecture carried the reason directly, such as F17/F18 server tick and no-last-write-wins. Affective exception whys around social restraint, notification refusal, and relationship framing often became rule-only rows.
- **Weight-3 vs weight-2.** Higher-weight whys did better (70.0/116.0, 60.3%) than weight-2 whys (14.0/32.0, 43.8%), because several load-bearing architecture whys were explicit. Still, many weight-3 rows were only partial because the downstream consequence layer was compressed away.
- **System-level vs feature-level.** System-level fidelity was much stronger (22.0/28.0, 78.6%) than feature-level fidelity (62.0/120.0, 51.7%). The planner preserved philosophy and architecture more effectively than feature-specific rationale.
- **Multi-layer recovery patterns.** Primary causes usually survived; secondary and downstream layers dropped more often. Common losses were the exact affective consequence (for example S2's processed-vs-seen distinction) and the future-proofing rationale (for example F16's retrofit warning and F27's foothold/slippery-slope layer).
- **Subdomain patterns.** Simulation, sync, and audio were comparatively faithful. Social optional features, account export/deletion, notebook editability, and several accessibility/performance rationales were more likely to become implementation checklists without the intended why.
- **Evidence-bound effects.** The v06 evidence gate denied credit for plausible but missing rationales such as F26, F29, F34, F35, F37, F38, and F39. These had plan/reconstruction rule evidence but not the gold reason.
- **Borderline capture.** Capture was leaned inclusive for settle definition, mood fast-timescale framing, bird identity, background-tab behavior, weather-to-mood, visits default-off, visitor actual-state, and 60fps target device qualification. These did not dominate the score, but they explain why planning quality is high while fidelity is moderate.

What the failure shape suggests: this candidate is a strong implementation planner and a decent philosophy compressor, but the reconstruction frequently collapses product intent into build mechanics. The largest benchmark signal is not that the model misses the app; it misses why several quiet refusals must stay refusals.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Add more feature-level whys for account/social quietness in v2. This run captured many social/account rules but lost their relationship rationale, especially F34-F39.
- Preserve the evidence-bound operator. It exposed a real difference between rule capture and why recovery; without it, F26 and the social/account rows would look better than they deserve.
- Consider making system-level downstream layers easier to identify or requiring explicit layer notes from reconstructors. S1, S2, S6, and S9 show that B often recovered the principle but not the consequence.
- Keep targeted headroom additions. F29-F40 were useful: several were captured as implementation details but failed recovery, which is exactly the saturation-reduction behavior those additions were meant to produce.
- For future instances, include a few more functional whys with relational consequences. F16/F20/F35 show that functional privacy/account features can still test whether the model preserves product intent rather than just implementation.

## 5. Methodology caveats

- **Fresh-context fidelity.** I treated phase 2A as frozen and did not modify it. The validity audit found no obvious gold-ID or rubric leakage.
- **Single-run-at-temperature limitation.** This is one candidate and one reconstruction pass; it gives no variance signal by itself.
- **Borderline capture calls.** I leaned inclusive on eight features where a rule was implicit or compressed. The largest impact is planning quality; fidelity remains governed by exact why evidence.
- **System-level cross-cutting.** The >=3-inheritance bar was met for all system whys, but S6 and S9 still scored partial because layer recovery was incomplete.
- **Confabulation cases.** No material ungrounded reconstruction claims drove positive credit; ungrounded_reconstruction_count is 0.
- **Evidence-bound denials.** The major denials were rule-without-why rows and plan-only missing layers, not lack of feature capture.
- **Rule-without-why cases.** There were 12 included rule-without-why cases, concentrated around hidden mood/motion rationale, no streak/social surfaces, account export/deletion, and sharing/visit-log rationale.
