## 3. Diagnostic patterns

- **Implementation coverage was much stronger than rationale preservation.** The plan captured 64/120 features, especially engine, audio, accessibility, and performance mechanics. The frozen reconstruction often recovered the same mechanics but not the reason those mechanics mattered.
- **Accessibility was the clearest preservation zone.** S9 scored full because the plan and reconstruction both kept accessibility in v1 and tied it to naturalist narration, reduced motion, captions, and testing.
- **Affective refusals leaked heavily.** S2, F10, F11, F14, F22, F27, F38, and F40 show the main pattern: a rule may be present or implied, but the benchmark rationale about attention loops, announcements, obligation, or queue overload is missing.
- **Weight-3 whys did not reliably survive.** Many high-weight entries recovered only layer 1: F1/F13 recovered the presence conjunction, F2 recovered low-pass calibration, F24 recovered naturalist narration, and F25 recovered cross-fades, while downstream consequences disappeared.
- **Server-side sync was the strongest functional cluster.** S7, F17, and F18 all earned partial credit because canonical server state, tick ownership, and no last-write-wins were present. The lost part was the failure-mode explanation.
- **Privacy/account lifecycle was nearly absent.** S8, F20, F34, F35, and F36 mostly became unreachable or none because export, deletion, aggregate telemetry, and per-bird privacy boundaries did not appear in the plan.
- **Evidence-bound scoring mattered.** Several plausible v1-style recoveries were denied or capped because the reconstruction offered a build rule, not a why: F6, F14, F16, F21, F23, F26, F27, F31, F32, F37, and F40.

What this suggests: the candidate compressed the PRD into a conventional implementation plan. That was enough to preserve many buildable surfaces, but not enough to encode the product's delicate intent boundaries.

## 4. Recommendations for v2 hardening

- Keep the evidence-bound operator. It cleanly separated feature capture from why recovery and exposed the main failure shape: mechanism without rationale.
- Add more targeted headroom around privacy and account lifecycle. This run skipped export, deletion, aggregate telemetry, and per-bird privacy almost entirely; those areas are useful discriminators.
- Preserve multi-layer whys, but consider making layer labels more explicit in reports. Most partials recovered layer 1 only; a dashboard slice by layer would make the loss easier to compare across runs.
- Keep the system-level cross-cutting bar, but require scorers to distinguish "principle present" from "rationale present." S3/S4/S5 were borderline because naturalist/compact rules survived without the deeper why.
- Add a small diagnostic for "named non-goal only." The plan often listed non-goals without the reason they are dangerous; this is distinct from both absent capture and full rationale recovery.
